## What this is

T7 generates questions from the dimension model; T8 extracts values from free
text. Neither is an *interview*. An interview has sequencing, follow-ups that
depend on what was said, coverage it drives towards, and a manner.

The brief (`status/plan.md`, Scope extension §3), from the repository owner:

- **the last job** — what they did there, whether it went well or badly, and why;
- **interests, successes, failures** — failures as material to learn from;
- **personality** — creative? ambitious? what they do with their spare time;
  whether they study their field outside work;
- **the first-job branch** — none of the retrospective questions apply, and
  asking them anyway tells a first-time candidate the tool is not for them;
- **empathic throughout** — take the positive, turn the negative into a lesson.

## The measurable half of manner

Empathy is a manner and mostly unmeasurable. One part is not: a question that
follows a negative episode must be a lesson-extraction follow-up, not a further
probe into the failure. `negative_episode_followup_rate == 1.0` is a real gate,
and it is the difference between an interview and an interrogation.

## Coverage

`interview_profile_coverage` = the fraction of profile fields a scripted
respondent's session fills — T24's candidate attributes, the trait dimensions
from T26, and ≥1 story-bank episode per elicited dimension. Measured against a
recorded transcript fixture, so it runs without a human in the loop.

## Open, decide before claiming

- One long session, or several sittings with resumable state?
- Are traits scored continuously, or held as episodes until enough accumulate to
  justify a value?

Both were left open in the scope extension. Ask before building.

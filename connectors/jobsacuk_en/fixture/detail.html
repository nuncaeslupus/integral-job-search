About the role

The Department of Biology, University of Oxford, is seeking a Research Assistant (G6) to support ResIntegrate, an ERC Consolidator Grant led by Prof. Rob Salguero-Gómez. ResIntegrate aims to develop an integrative theory of ecological resilience, examine its macroecology, and test how resilience scales from individuals to populations and communities under interacting disturbances. The post holder will contribute to research using global demographic databases and the DRAGNet grassland network, with emphasis on data curation, ecological analysis and reproducible research.

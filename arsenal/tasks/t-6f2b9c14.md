## The defect

A floor exists so that a clean zero over an empty or shrunken scan cannot pass
as a measurement. Three shipped floors do not do that, and were found by an AST
sweep during T122's review rather than by anything that runs:

| module | floor | population | effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| `profile.MINIMUM_FIELDS_CHECKED` | `len(_D6_FIXTURE)` (`:930`) | `len(_D6_FIXTURE)` (`:990`) | the comparison is `len(X) < len(X)` — **false for every X, including the empty tuple.** The floor has never fired and cannot. |
| `bodyless_post.MINIMUM_PROBES` | `8` (`:78`) | `len(PROBES) == 9` | the first deleted probe is tolerated silently; no margin argued |
| `page_placeholder.MINIMUM_PROBES` | `21` (`:68`) | `len(PROBES) == 25` | four deletions tolerated silently; no margin argued |

`profile`'s is the pure case and the reason to state the rule as a property
rather than a number: **a bound derived from the thing it bounds is not a
bound.** T122 met the same shape from the other side — `MINIMUM_ARRANGEMENTS_PROBED`
was `len(ARRANGEMENTS)` compared against a population of `len(ARRANGEMENTS) + 1`,
so the first deletion breached nothing — and its fix deliberately keeps the
floor a **literal**, because written `len(ARRANGEMENTS) + 1` both sides shrink
together and the guard can never fire at all.

`salary_recovery.MINIMUM_WORDING_CASES` (120 against 153) is the **contrast, not
a fourth member**: its comment argues the margin and records raising it. That is
what a deliberate distance looks like.

## The rule this task has to enforce

Every committed floor either **sits where the first deletion breaches it**, or
**states the margin it keeps and why**. Nothing else. A floor whose distance from
its population is an accident is a floor that reports a measurement it did not
make.

## Sweeping is the hard half, and three blind spots are already known

Do not re-derive the search — three separate readings found three different holes
in it, and any of them re-opens the class:

* the `MINIMUM_*` / `MIN_*` name filter **misses this repo's `*_AT_LEAST` and
  `*_MINIMUM` spellings** — 22 bounds outside it, ten unambiguously floors;
* the candidate set was formed by an **equality fingerprint**, which by
  construction cannot find a floor carrying slack — *the entire class in
  question*. Found by the implementer against its own method, which is why it is
  written down here;
* a floor whose comparison is **delegated to a helper never appears at a
  comparison site**. `review_reader` has four such floors, invisible to both
  sweeps run so far.

So the sweep is over **declaration and use together**, not over names, and its
own completeness is part of what the gate measures.

## What the metric counts

`floors_that_do_not_refuse_the_first_deletion` is the number of committed floors
for which removing one member of the population it guards does **not** produce a
breach, and whose margin is not argued in writing beside it.

The floors swept are the denominator, committed as a **floor** in the
`naming.MINIMUM_SCANNED` style (T100) — and it must be a **literal**, per T122's
own finding: derived from the sweep's own result it shrinks with any floor the
sweep stops finding, which is this task's defect committed inside this task's
gate.

**Verify the metric is non-zero against today's tree before writing the fix** —
it should find at least the three above. A gate satisfied by doing nothing is the
defect this repository keeps meeting.

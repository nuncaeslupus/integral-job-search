## Where this comes from

The DOMMA CV needed a photo — in Spain a CV without one reads as incomplete.
The candidate had already handed over a CV PDF at intake, with her photo in it.
Asking her to find and send a JPEG would have been asking for something already
in the profile store.

`pdfimages -list` names the embedded images and `pdfimages -png -f <page>` pulls
them out; the largest image on page 1 is the photo in every CV layout met so
far. It goes into the rendered HTML as a base64 `data:` URI (T181), which is
what keeps the deliverable one file.

## What to build

Extraction at **intake**, not at application time: when a candidate's source CV
lands in the store, pull any embedded photo and record it as a profile asset.
Then step-11 uses it without a round trip to the candidate.

Heuristic and its ceiling, stated rather than hidden: largest raster image on
the first page, minimum ~200px on the short side, aspect ratio between 0.6 and
1.4. A logo on a designed CV can beat that; when two candidates fail it, the
answer is to show the candidate what was found and let them veto, not to add a
fourth rule.

## Tests

`test_photo_is_extracted_from_a_source_cv` over a fixture PDF.

`test_a_logo_only_pdf_extracts_nothing` — no photo is a normal outcome, not an
error, and must not produce a logo in the header.

Review leftovers from #974 (refuse current pay stated by an earning verb), batched per policy G after its second review round. Full cases are in the round-2 second-reader report on #974.

Fail-open in `integral.pay_transparency.states_own_pay`. Each returns sendable and must be refused:

1. Earning verb in one sentence, number in the next: "I earn well. It is 38.", "Gano bien. Son 38.", "Guanyo bé. Són 38."
2. Earning verbs missing from `_EARNS`, with a bare number: "Me pagan 38.", "Em paguen 38.", "Percibo 38."
3. A time word with a bare number (`_WHEN` is never reached without a money figure): "Actualmente 38.", "Currently on 38.", "I am on 38.", "Ara 38."
4. Rate words: "My rate is 9 an hour.", "Hourly rate: 25.", "Day rate 300.", "Tarifa diaria 300."
5. Still open from #971: "Looking for 38k, same as I have.", "Busco 38k, como en mi puesto.", "Mi sueldo mínimo era 38k.", "My salary minimum was 38k."

Fail-closed (sendable text refused):

6. New in #974, the earning-verb-plus-digit clause refuses achievements that mention no pay: "I make 3 releases a week.", "I make 10 deploys a day.", "Made 3 PRs per day.", "I made 4 hires in 2023.", "I earned 2 certifications.", "Earned a promotion in 2022.", "Cobro desde 2019 en la empresa.", "Guanyo 2 hores al dia en desplaçaments."
7. Expectations refused since #971/#974: "de 45 a 50k", "from 45k to 50k", "45k is my minimum.", "Entre 45 y 50k.", "Busco 45k más bonus de 5k", "I'd like to earn 45k".

Also left from #971: letters, CVs and interview scripts get no free-text pay sweep.

## Wanted

Close 1-5 with closed rules rather than more words (judge pay across adjacent sentences; a bare number after an earning or time word counts as pay unless a unit noun follows it or it is a four-digit year); fix 6 with that same unit-noun / year test. Every case above becomes a test in ES, EN and CA.

> 7 is weird. Given the same job, always better more money.

Step 6 asked the candidate to rate something on a scale where one end was
incoherent. Holding everything else equal, more pay is never worse, so a
mid-scale answer is not a preference — it is the candidate trying to be
cooperative with a malformed question, and `weights.py` then fits a part-worth
to noise.

The general fault: an elicitation item whose dimension is **monotone** cannot be
asked as a level rating. What is elicitable is the *trade-off* — how much of
something else this pay is worth — which is what a part-worth means in the first
place.

Cheapest correct fix is a property of the question bank, not of `weights.py`: a
dimension declared monotone may not produce a level-rating item.

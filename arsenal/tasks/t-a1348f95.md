### Finding

### Evidence
grep of urlopen/requests call sites and the def outline of tools/collect_ads.py; connectors/getmanfred_es, tecnoempleo_es, remotive_en, weworkremotely_en exist alongside.

### Suggested direction
One transport module (see the missing live fetcher) used by all four, and have collect_ads read boards through the connector packages.

## What this is

Date of birth, address, telephone, the name to print — asked **here**, for the
document being produced, not gathered speculatively at Intake months earlier.
They do not improve a search; they are required by the document, so the document
is where they are asked for.

And the send boundary: the tool prepares the files, the text to paste into the
employer's form, or an email left in drafts. The candidate presses send.

## The rule that matters

**A story-bank episode reaches an employer-bound document only with per-use
approval.** Recounting a failure to the tool was never consent to send it to a
company.

**No autonomous outward action.** If sending is ever built, the gate is a
summary of everything that would go — which documents, which claims, which
contact details, to whom — approved once per application, never as a standing
permission. Stopping one step short of sending is not a limitation; it keeps the
last decision with the person whose name is on the application.

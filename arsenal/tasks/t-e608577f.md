Imported from issue #528

`tools/excerpt_fixture.py` truncates an advert body by locating its container with a `<div>`-only pattern:

```python
re.finditer(r'<div(?=[\s>])[^>]*?\sclass="([^"]*)"[^>]*>', raw)
```

and `tests/test_excerpt_fixture.py`'s `_div_class_lists` / `_DivClasses(HTMLParser)` co-restrict to `<div>` as well, so the test and the tool share the assumption rather than the test checking it. A board serving its body in a `<section>`, `<article>` or `<p>` gets **no span located — and an unmatched span is left whole**, which is the fail-open direction: the tool reports success and the fixture ships the full advert.

This is pre-existing, found while fixing the attribute-order half of the same locator on #520 (T194) and deliberately left out of that diff.

**There is a candidate instance already committed.** `connectors/nofluffjobs_en/meta.yaml` declares `bodies: excerpted` and `fixture/list.html` carries no ellipsis marker — so either the tool was never run on it or it ran and located nothing. Worth measuring before assuming which.

Two things the fix should do, and the second is the one that makes it a check rather than a description:

1. locate the span by the element the connector's own selector names, not by a hard-coded tag;
2. make "located nothing" a **failure** of the tool rather than a silent pass — the current behaviour is indistinguishable from "nothing needed cutting", which is the same shape as rule 7's own fail-open family in `connector_contract.py`.

A gate along the lines of `excerpted_fixtures_without_a_marker == 0` over the committed library would catch the `nofluffjobs_en` case and any future one.

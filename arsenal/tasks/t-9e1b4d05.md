## The defect

Found as **C10** by the second reader on #409, reproduced by it against
`origin/main` byte-identically (`swept=False sendable=True findings=0`), and
correctly left out of T114's diff as pre-existing and out of scope.

The state: the manifest's episode row is **removed**, the per-use approval is
**kept**, the episode's own line is deleted from the letter, and the CV headline
carries the substance. The draft is **sendable** and `payload.json` reports
`episode_disclosures: 0` — the story is described to the candidate as withheld
while a headline is about to carry it to the employer.

**This is not an approval defect and must not be fixed as one.** The
`episode.text in approved` exemption predates T114, the approval genuinely names
that text for that version, and §6.2's per-use approval is therefore satisfied:
the candidate *did* agree this story may go out. Nothing here should start
blocking a send that §6.2 permits.

What is wrong is narrower and is entirely about the summary. `approval.py`'s own
docstring says the module exists so that the tool does not describe a document it
is not looking at:

> that one is about substance reaching an employer, this one is about the tool
> describing a document it is not looking at

`payload.json`'s `claims` and `episodes` are assembled from the **manifest's
rows**. `documents` is read from disk. So one field of the payload is measured
and the others are asserted, and the candidate confirms all of them under one
digest. A manifest edit is enough to reach the divergence.

## What a fix must and must not do

* **Must:** make the payload's account of what is disclosed agree with the
  documents it names. The candidate confirms *this* payload by its digest, so the
  payload has to be true about the files on disk at the moment it is written.
* **Must not:** refuse a send whose disclosure §6.2's per-use approval covers.
  Over-refusing here is not free — it costs a regeneration, and a draft blocked
  for content the candidate already approved is the over-refusal the padded
  shingle match was introduced to stop.
* **Weight fail-open over fail-closed** in the *reporting*: a payload that
  overstates what is disclosed is a candidate reading a scarier summary than the
  truth; a payload that understates it is a candidate confirming a send they were
  told does not contain something it contains. Only the second is the harm this
  module exists to refuse.
* Say in the code which of the two the choice takes and why, next to the choice.

## What the metric counts

`payloads_reporting_a_carried_story_as_withheld` is the number of constructed
states in which a generated document carries an episode's substance and the
payload written for that version does not account for it — regardless of whether
an approval backs it, because the defect is the description and not the
permission.

The states evaluated are the denominator, asserted as a **floor** in the
`naming.MINIMUM_SCANNED` style (T100), so a control list that shrank to nothing
cannot score a serene zero. **Verify the metric is non-zero under today's
unmodified behaviour before writing the fix** — a gate satisfied by doing nothing
is the defect this repository keeps meeting.

Include a control in which an approved, manifest-declared, document-carried
episode still sends cleanly, so a fix cannot buy the metric by refusing more.

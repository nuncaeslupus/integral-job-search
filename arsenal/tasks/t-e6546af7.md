Found during the test session of 2026-08-20 (candidate: a Barcelona construction worker).

`extraction.extract()` settled **0 of 25 dimensions on all seven adverts**. `dimensions/` is entirely
software-sector: `stack_modernity`, `on_call_load`, `english_demand`, `product_vs_services`,
`technical_depth`. For a bricklayer the whole of step 8 is inert, so step 9 ranked by hand — which the
skill explicitly forbids ("never a paragraph assembled by a model").

This is not a bug in `extract`; the vocabulary does not cover the market. Decide deliberately:
a sector-scoped dimension set, or an honest refusal when no dimension applies. What must not stand is
step 8 reporting success over a vocabulary that matched nothing.

Addressed in: `dimensions/`, `src/integral/extraction.py`, `step-08-understanding`.

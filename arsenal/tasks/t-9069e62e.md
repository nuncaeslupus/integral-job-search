From a test-mode note captured 2026-09-06 at step `sourcing`:

> When the user discards a job with a reason (like here with "yo no soy applied reearcher"), this info must be stored like gold, not skipped as just a comment. It is part of their profile, something to discard or add jobs to the list to be shown. As you already do, you must say: "4 jobs were discarded because, although they talked about agentic AI, they are about research, which is not your field".

The candidate said it in this session — *"Applied Research Scientist, eso no sería mi perfil"* — and it was used, once, in the reply, and then lost. Nothing was written to `profile/evidence.jsonl`, so the next session will show him research roles again and he will have to say it a second time.

A reason given while rejecting an offer is the cheapest evidence this tool ever gets: unprompted, specific, and about a real advert the candidate has just read. It is worth more than an answer to a question, because nobody asked for it.

Three things have to happen and only the first is hard:

1. **The reason becomes an evidence row**, attributed to the offer that provoked it, so it is reviewable and correctable like every other recorded decision (§5.7 — a refusal is evidence and is not permanent).
2. **It filters the next sourcing run** — offers matching it are withheld rather than shown.
3. **The withheld count is reported, with its reason**, in the form the owner wrote: *"4 descartadas porque, aunque hablan de agentic AI, son de investigación, que no es tu campo."* A silent filter is indistinguishable from a thin market, which is the failure `step-07-sourcing` already names for liveness.

The gate is on the first and third: a reason given and not stored is the fail-open direction, and a filter that withholds without saying so is the one that makes the tool look broken.

Two numbers, and the second is the one that keeps the first honest:
`discard_reasons_not_stored` counts rejections carrying a stated reason that
produced no evidence row, and `silently_withheld_offers` counts offers a
stored reason filtered out without the count and the reason being reported
back. A filter nobody is told about is indistinguishable from a thin market,
which is the failure `step-07-sourcing` already names for liveness.

Both are fail-open by construction: a reason not stored is asked for again
next session, and a silent filter is a candidate wondering why so little came
back.

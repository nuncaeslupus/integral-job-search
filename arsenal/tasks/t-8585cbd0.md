## Why

The gate produces verdicts; this is where the candidate sees them. An **Excluded** section lists what was removed and the sentence it was removed for; a `FLAG` offer stays in the ranked list carrying its marker.

**The count is reported, never silently dropped.** This is the rule D-18 already established for withheld offers: quietly dropping four of nine looks identical to nine having been found, and the candidate then wonders why so little came back.

Frontier mathematics is untouched — excluded offers never enter the dominance comparison at all.

## What it must not become

Do not let an exclusion reach the frontier as a very low score. Dominance over a barred offer is meaningless, and D-17's lesson applies to the card: render the template, do not let the model assemble the prose.

**A zero-violation count over an empty input set is not a pass.** The gate counts
violations, and nothing counted is also zero — so the evidence record must carry
`excluded_offers_shown_without_a_reason_evaluated` (ranked runs rendered) and the gate is only meaningful while that count is
non-zero. This is the failure this whole increment is about, turned on its own gates:
a check that reports success over work it did not do. Assert the denominator.

`approval.measure_prepared` joins `surviving` into `intact` with
`"\n".join(...)` before searching it for an episode's substance. That is the
same join #435 round four (T156) removed from the sibling `unbacked` scan
(F1): two lines that are never adjacent on any real page — split across
`cv.md` and `letter.md`, or simply non-adjacent within one file — can still
land next to each other in the joined string, and a normalised eight-word
shingle can straddle the manufactured seam.

Here the direction is the opposite of F1's, which is why it was left open on
round four and again on round five (R5-1): a shingle manufactured only by the
join, naming no real episode substance on the page, adds an unearned finding
in `_carries(intact, episode.text)` and blocks an otherwise-clean draft —
fail-**closed**, costing a regeneration rather than letting anything through.
Round five's second reader verified the direction by measurement rather than
taking it on the module docstring's claim, and accepted leaving the fix out of
that round on the one condition that it get this row and this task file rather
than only a docstring paragraph nothing tests.

The remedy the docstring already names: check each `surviving` line
individually, the same discipline `unbacked` now uses
(`... for line in unbacked if line not in approved`), so `intact` closes the
same way — `any(_carries(line, episode.text) for line in surviving)` in place
of `_carries(intact, episode.text)`, with `intact` retired once nothing reads
it.

## What the metric counts

`intact_seam_manufactured_findings` is the number of constructed states in
which two `surviving` lines that are never adjacent on any one page — split
across `cv.md`/`letter.md`, or non-adjacent within one document — join to
manufacture an eight-word shingle naming no episode actually on the page, and
`measure_prepared` reports a finding for the episode that shingle names
anyway. Mirrors T156's own seam fixture (`seam_head`/`seam_tail`/
`seam_episode`) exactly, but planted among **approved, disclosed** lines
(`surviving`) rather than unbacked ones, since that is the population `intact`
draws from.

The denominator is the states evaluated, floored per T100's convention so an
enumeration that shrank to nothing cannot score a clean zero.

Imported from issue #566

Found by the second reader on #565 (F4), while reviewing the change that makes foreign boards reachable at all.

`_violates_salary` in `src/integral/candidate.py`:

```python
if offer.salary_currency is None or offer.salary_currency != field_value.currency:
    return None  # cannot compare across currencies without a conversion rate
```

There is no conversion anywhere in `src/integral/`. So every advert quoting a band in a currency the candidate did not state their floor in passes the salary check unexamined — neither rejected nor flagged, just silently not compared.

## Why this matters now

It was already reachable through USD on the `GLOBAL` boards. #565 widens it to GBP, PLN and every other currency the foreign national boards quote — and **the candidate's stated reason for wanting those boards is the pay comparison**: local roles at 30–60k EUR against foreign ones that often start at 100k USD. The one comparison the whole change exists to enable is the one the filter declines to make.

The failure is quiet in both directions:

- a 40k USD role clears a 60k EUR floor it does not meet;
- and nothing in the summary says the comparison was skipped, so the number is read as though it had been checked.

## What the rule should be

Unknown must not fabricate a hard-constraint result — that is the rule the whole module is built on, and returning `None` is right *as a veto*. What is missing is that the offer is then presented as though its pay had been checked. Two candidate directions, and the first is much cheaper:

1. **Say so.** Mark the offer as pay-uncompared and surface that in the ranking and the summary, the same way `unplaced` (#547) withholds an offer whose location cannot be read rather than accepting it.
2. **Convert**, with a stored, dated rate and the rate quoted beside the comparison, so a candidate can see what the number was converted at.

The last three are the ones today's rule passes silently.

Pin the opposite direction too: a same-currency comparison must still veto, and a missing band must still not veto.

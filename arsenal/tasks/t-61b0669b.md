Source: Manfred Tech Career Report 2026, on the EU pay-transparency directive (2023/970), which Spain must transpose by June 2026: employers must give the pay range before the interview, and may not ask a candidate's current or past pay.

## Wanted

Step 11 (`step-11-application`) and step 12 (`step-12-interview-log`) say so when it applies:

- If the advert publishes no pay range, step 11 tells the candidate they can ask for it before the first interview, and step 12's preparation includes the question.
- Neither step ever writes the candidate's current or past pay into a document, a form answer or an interview script; a form question asking for it is answered with the expected range only, from the candidate's stated floor.
- Same behaviour and wording coverage in Spanish, English and Catalan.

## Tests

`tests/test_pay_transparency.py`: an advert with no range triggers the prompt in all three languages; an advert with a range does not; a form answer drafted for "current salary" cites no current-pay evidence row and is refused if it would.

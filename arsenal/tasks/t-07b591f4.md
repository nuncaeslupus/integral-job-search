Learned in a candidate session at step application (2026-10-21).

Candidates filling a profile site (Manfred, LinkedIn and similar) need, per employer, school and certificate: a square logo in one consistent style and the field texts (title, issuer, dates, credential URL, description). Doing it by hand took most of a session.

Scope:
- From cv/master.json (jobs, education) and certificate rows, list every entity that needs a logo and texts.
- Logo: find a source (Wikimedia Commons SVG first, then the entity's own site, then the Wayback Machine for defunct sites), trim the margin, fit the longest side to 420 px and centre it on a 512x512 white canvas. Never distort the aspect ratio; keep marks such as TM. A logo that cannot be found is reported, never invented.
- Texts: one block per field, in the site's language, drawn only from store entries.
- Certificates: list specializations, not the courses inside them; formal studies go in Education, MOOCs only in Certificates.
- Output goes to the candidate's profile, `profile/logos/` (`issuers/` for employers, schools and certificate issuers; `projects/` for their own projects), never to send/. Keep an SVG beside the PNG when the logo was drawn or found as a vector.

Done when one command produces the folder for a fixture profile and a test pins the 512/420 geometry and the aspect ratio.

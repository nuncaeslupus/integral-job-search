Review leftovers from #971 (refuse any pay figure that is not an expectation or a floor), batched per policy G after its second review round. Full cases are in the round-2 second-reader report on #971.

Fail-open in `integral.pay_transparency.states_own_pay`. Each returns sendable and must be refused:

1. The range rule drops a current figure as a range's low end: "Busco pasar de 38k a 45k", "Vull passar de 38k a 45k", "Expecting a raise from 38k to 45k".
2. A want word before a figure that is the current salary: "Looking for more than the 38k I make", "Espero mantener mi sueldo de 38k", "Vull mantenir el meu sou de 38k".
3. Past pay by tense, with no marker word: "Busco lo mismo que cobraba en 2023: 38k", "Minimum is what I was paid in 2022, 38k", "Busco el que cobrava, 38k".
4. Figures the money pattern misses: "Busco 45k, cobro 38", "Busco 45k i cobro 38", "Looking for 45k, I make 38 at the moment", "Cobro 1800 netos al mes", "Cobro 1800 nets al mes", "I take home 2500 net a month", "Gano 38 al año".

Fail-closed (an expectation refused):

5. "I'd like 45k", "45k is my minimum", "Mi expectativa actualizada es 45k" (`actual\w*` matches "actualizada"), "Busco 45k más bonus de 5k".

Also left from #971: case 5 of the original task (letters, CVs and interview scripts get no free-text pay sweep).

## Wanted

Close 1-4 with one rule rather than more words: refuse a sentence with an earning verb (cobro, cobraba, gano, ganaba, cobrava, guanyo, earn, make, made, paid, take home) anywhere in it, or a possessive of pay with no want word governing it ("mi sueldo de 38k", but "my ideal salary is 45k" stays sendable); do not read "de/from <figure> a/to" as a range; let any word sit between a number and its pay period. Every case above becomes a test in ES, EN and CA.

Imported from issue #567

Found by the second reader on #565 (F2).

`sourcing.packages_for` builds three buckets and returns early twice:

```python
_WORLDWIDE_REACH = frozenset({"remote", "cross_border_remote_employer"})
...
if not reaches_worldwide(constraints):
    return domestic
...
if not reaches_across_borders(constraints):
    return domestic + worldwide
```

`relocate` is in neither predicate's set. So a candidate whose reach is exactly `("relocate",)` — someone who has said in as many words that they would move country for the right job — returns at the **first** guard and is offered their own country's boards and nothing else. The foreign bucket the whole of #565 adds is never reached, and the `unreached_because` caption tells them their reach "does not include remote work", which is true and is not the reason.

It is fail-open in the direction that matters least for safety and most for the search: nothing forbidden is fetched, but the adverts the candidate is most likely to want are the ones never looked for.

## Why it is not just a one-line widening

The two predicates are nested, and that nesting is load-bearing: `reaches_worldwide` gates a bucket of `GLOBAL` boards (remote-first job boards), `reaches_across_borders` gates other countries' own national boards. `relocate` belongs to the **second** and not the first — a relocating candidate wants Germany's boards, not necessarily a remote-only aggregator. So adding `relocate` to `_WORLDWIDE_REACH` would be wrong; what is needed is for the first guard to stop being the gate on the second.

Restructure so each bucket is gated by its own predicate rather than by a chain, and every `ReachMode` in the `Literal` reaches at least one bucket.

`reach_modes_reaching_no_board_beyond_the_candidates_own_country == 0`, over a population **derived from `ReachMode`'s own `Literal` args** rather than listed — so a mode added later is covered without anyone remembering to — for every non-empty subset up to size 2. Pin the opposite direction in the same measurement: a reach of exactly `("commute",)` must still return the domestic bucket alone, or the gate is "offer everything" wearing a fix's clothes.

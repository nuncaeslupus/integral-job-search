Filed from a candidate session (test-mode 658fcce2).

`sourcing_exclusions.matches` looks for the value and every term anywhere in title, employer and text. Seen in session 658fcce2: a payroll startup held as `sector:fintech` because the advert values a "Background in FinTech"; a firmware engineer held as `sector:advertising`; and `restaurante`/`restaurant` matching 58 adverts through "ticket restaurant" among the perks. The topic has to be about the employer or the job, not any sentence of the advert — and whatever is held must still be named to the candidate with the words that matched.

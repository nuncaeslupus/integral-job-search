## Why

`applications/{offer_id}/` records what was sent and when, and has no vocabulary for what happened next — so 'what became of this application' is not a thing the tool can state.

Canonical, underscored (`status/spec-v3-silent-success.md` §5.2):

| Status | Class |
|---|---|
| `drafted`, `applied`, `interview`, `offer` | Open |
| `hired`, `rejected`, `no_response`, `offer_declined`, `withdrawn` | Final |

Legacy space-spellings are **accepted on read, never written** — tolerance at the boundary, not a rewrite of stored records.

**Why this is in scope while Phase 7 is deferred:** it is the schema Option C's outcome calibration reads. Dropping it as 'Phase 7, not now' is the one thing in this increment that would foreclose the increment after it.

## What it must not become

A vocabulary's entire value is in being fixed, so do not add a status because a case seems unhandled — raise it instead.

**Never infer `hired` or `offer_declined`.** Accepting or declining is the candidate's decision and is recorded only when they say so. This matters now because it is the rule the deferred mail sensor will inherit.

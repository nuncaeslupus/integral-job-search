Imported from issue #723

These are the findings from the second reader on #720 that did not block the merge, plus one decision for the owner.

- **O2: detail pages are fetched before the reach check.** A row's detail page is fetched, and spends the detail budget, before the reach check runs. A row that turns out to be out of reach has already used it.
- **O3: no evidence measures the default share.** The four gates now pass `board_cap=OFFER_CEILING`, so `measure_flood` measures something other than the default behaviour that ships. No evidence key exercises `over_board_cap` or `out_of_reach`, and the T251/T253 plan metric has no evidence file. Add a per-board version of `requests_after_the_ceiling`.
- **Resume starts every share fresh.** Calling `source(offset=…)` on its own gives every board a new share.
- **Owner decision:** a board's share is spent mostly on its first term, so the later terms are starved. They are now named as "never searched for". Should the share be split per term, or should boards walk the terms round-robin?

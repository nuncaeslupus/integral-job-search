Imported from issue #557

`es.talent.com` renders a sidebar of *other* listings inside the advert page, and the connector stores it as part of this advert's body.

Measured over the 266 offers of the 2026-09-21/22 new-method batch: **1 offer** (Prima, `Software Engineer (Python)`) carries another advert's text, and it is **18% of that body**.

Rare, and it already produced a wrong claim to a candidate. The salary "€45,000.00 - €60,000.00" read out of that body belongs to a Madrid listing in the sidebar, not to the Barcelona vacancy the body is filed under, and it was stated to the candidate as this employer's band before the source was traced.

Anything that reads the body is exposed the same way: salary recovery, the dimension cue rules, keyword relevance filters, the reading digest. A cue matched in the sidebar scores this advert on another employer's words.

Suggested shape: the connector strips the related-listings block at capture, so no downstream reader has to know. A body-level heuristic in each consumer is the enumeration this repo keeps paying for — the fix belongs where the page structure is still available.

Detection used here, as a starting point rather than a rule: sidebar rows carry a recency stamp (`10 months ago`, `hace 3 días`) immediately beside a location and a band, which the advert's own prose does not.

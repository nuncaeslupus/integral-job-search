Source: Manfred Tech Career Report 2026, which separates offers by who publishes them (product company, consultancy, recruitment agency). A candidate who rules out consultancies and temp agencies (ETT) still sees their adverts, because the advert names the agency or nothing at all.

## Wanted

First read what `search/exclusions.json` and `integral.sourcing_exclusions` already filter, and do not duplicate it. Then classify each offer's publisher as `direct`, `consultancy`, `agency` or `unknown` from the advert text: phrases such as "para importante cliente", "for our client", "per a un client", "empresa cliente", "incorporación a cliente final", in Spanish, English and Catalan with the same coverage. `unknown` is the default; a guess is never `direct`. A candidate who has ruled out consultancies or agencies gets those offers counted and named in the run's EXCLUDED line, like any other exclusion.

## Tests

`tests/test_publisher_kind.py`: each cue classifies in all three languages; an advert with no cue is `unknown`; a product company describing its own clients ("our clients use the app") is not `consultancy`; the exclusion counts appear in the run line.

## Acceptance gate

```bash
uv run --extra dev pytest tests/test_publisher_kind.py -q
```

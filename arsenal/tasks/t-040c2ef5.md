Filed from a candidate session. A candidate wanted to send a CV to an employer through its open form, for no concrete role.

## What happened

Step 11 requires a shortlisted offer, so the session wrote a synthetic `Offer` by hand (`source: manual`, an invented title, the form's fields as the advert text) and a lifecycle record with `track_new_offer`, then called `choose`. It is the second time: an application through a community profile was stored the same way.

## What is missing

- One supported way to record an open application: employer, where it is sent, the form's questions, no advert text. A function, not a hand-written record.
- The record is told apart from an advert, so liveness, deduplication, ranking and the 'ruled out before' check do not treat it as a vacancy.
- Step 11 accepts it and says what differs: no advert to tailor against, the employer's own site is the reference, and a form answer takes the place of the letter.
- A gate that counts open applications stored as adverts.

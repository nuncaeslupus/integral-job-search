Source: Manfred Tech Career Report 2026 (https://www.getmanfred.com/tech-career-report), which measures that 21% of tech adverts are republished in at least three different months.

## Wanted

Sourcing records, per employer + normalised title, the first and last month the pair was seen and how many distinct months it appeared in. An offer whose pair has appeared in three or more distinct months is flagged `recurring` and the ranking card says so in plain words ("this advert has been republished since <month>"), in Spanish, English and Catalan alike. The flag is shown, never used to hide or demote the offer by itself.

A pair with no employer name is not tracked (it cannot be matched), and the run says how many were left untracked.

## Tests

`tests/test_recurring_adverts.py`: an advert seen in three distinct months is flagged; the same advert seen three times in one month is not; two employers with the same title are separate pairs; a missing employer is counted as untracked, not flagged; the card wording exists in ES/EN/CA.

## Acceptance gate

```bash
uv run --extra dev pytest tests/test_recurring_adverts.py -q
```

`pythonorg_en` sent `?page=1` and `?page=2` against a capture recording only
`https://www.python.org/jobs/`, so T113 dropped its pagination — costing **6 of
that board's 31 adverts**. The page-2 request is not speculative: it is the
board's own emitted href, it was exercised on 2026-08-30, and it answered 200
with six listings. What is missing is only the artefact.

Recording it needs one robots-adjudicated GET, and the session that tried could
not make it: this environment's egress proxy answers **403 to the CONNECT** for
python.org, which is an organisation policy denial and not a robots verdict.
The second reader verified that independently — curl reports `CONNECT tunnel
failed` for python.org and usajobs.gov while api.github.com returns 200.

Writing the capture from the dated sentence in the comment was refused, and
that refusal is right: a fabricated artefact certifying a request nobody
re-issued is T113's own defect committed under T113's name.

So this needs **a session with egress**, not a cleverer session. Adjudicate
with the repo's matcher first — never by reading robots.txt by eye — then make
one GET, commit the capture, and restore `pagination` for the package. The
metric exists because a board silently dropped for an unrecordable capture is
indistinguishable from a board that has no second page, and the difference is
six live adverts a candidate does not see.

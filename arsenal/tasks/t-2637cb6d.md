Raised by the second reader on #788 (T105), outside that diff (N1, fail-open, pre-existing).

`connector.yaml`'s copy of the capture date is read by the pydantic model (`ConnectorFile.last_verified`), not by `connector_contract._as_date`. It accepts `2026-08-30 00:00:00`, `2026-08-30T00:00:00Z` and `'2026-08-30T00:00:00'` with zero violations, by coercion. T105 made `meta.yaml` require a canonical plain date (a `date` that is not a `datetime`, or a string whose untrimmed text equals its `isoformat()`); the two files that "cannot differ" should share that grammar.

Also noted (N4, not reachable from YAML): a `date` subclass that is not a `datetime` is accepted.

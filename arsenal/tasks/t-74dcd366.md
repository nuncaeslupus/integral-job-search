Seven style corrections came out of one document round, and every one of them is
a rule rather than a one-off edit:

- not sounding as though he is asking to be quizzed on the employer's most
  basic course (*"Suena a que quiero que me pregunten por el curso más básico
  que tienen"*);
- *"without worrying whether they are perfectly structured"* →
  *"without worrying too much about their structure"*, because the first
  *"suena a mal trabajo"*;
- claiming authorship explicitly — *"I was the one who automated a good deal of
  my team tasks with AI"*, not *"I automated a good deal of it"*;
- no sentimental register; a paragraph opening *"It is also personal…"* was cut
  for exactly that;
- nothing unintelligible in the CV even when true — drawspec's eleven failure
  families were cut for it, and the fact itself is worth keeping for an
  interview, which is why the backfill stored it;
- no figures that go stale;
- less text and larger type over more content packed in, and no paragraph whose
  last line carries almost nothing (T143 measures that last one).

Every one was elicited by the candidate reading a draft and objecting. None of
them survives this application: the next one starts from the same defaults and
earns the same seven corrections, from a person who has already given them.

The backfill has now written them into `profile/evidence.jsonl` under
`step: "preferences"`, so they exist. **Nothing reads them.** That is this task:
generation consumes stored voice preferences, and a correction made once is not
asked for twice.

The harder half is what a voice preference is. It is not a dimension score —
scoring it against an advert means nothing. It is closer to a constraint on the
prose: stated once, applicable to every later document, checkable after the
fact. So it needs its own shape, and it needs to be **visible when applied** —
*"applied 6 of your stored preferences"*, with the list — because a preference
inferred from one irritated sentence can be wrong, and a wrong one that silently
governs every future document is worse than the correction it saved.

A retracted preference stops applying. `integral.retraction` already does the
suppression; the gate has to prove generation honours it.

## The defect

Both halves were found by the second reader on #455 (round 5), reported there
and deliberately kept out of that diff. Neither is a live fail-open; both are
places where a fixture reads as evidence and is not.

**A shape that moves nothing pins nothing.** `tests/test_liveness.py`'s
`_buried` shape exists to prove the block-marker scan reads the whole body and
not some prefix of it: it inserts ~24 KB of inert page text before the marker,
and a scan truncated at 2,000 characters then goes red. It anchors on
`<body>`, falling back to `<html>`. **The two Cloudflare samples carry their
markers in `<head>`**, before either anchor, so their buried offset stays at 19
where every other sample moves to ~24,400. Those two cells run, pass, and
assert nothing the "as recorded" cells did not already assert. The measured
one-line remedy is to reverse the anchor order to `("<html>", "<body>")`, at
which point all ten marker-bearing samples relocate — but the rule to enforce
is the property, not the tuple: **every (sample, shape) cell either moves the
thing it is shaped to move, or is reported.** A shape added later for a page
form nobody has met yet re-opens this by construction.

**A derived list with no floor.** `test_no_installed_board_as_a_browser_board_
reaches_the_plain_fetch` twins every installed GET package, which is the right
shape — a board added later is twinned without anyone remembering to — and it
is exactly the shape that can shrink to nothing and stay green: an empty
`_installed_get_packages()` collects as `1 skipped`, which pytest reports as
success. Confirmed directly by the reader. Today it covers 24 packages, 5 of
them ATS hosts with employers. `MINIMUM_PROBES_COMPARED = 12` and the
hand-written `lever_en` test mitigate it; neither asserts this population.

## What the metric counts

`fixture_shape_cells_that_relocate_nothing`: cells of the (refusal sample ×
shape) product whose shaping leaves the thing the shape exists to move where it
already was — for `buried`, the first marker's offset unchanged. The cells
compared are the denominator, committed as a **literal** floor in the
`naming.MINIMUM_SCANNED` style (T100/T122), so a product that shrank cannot
score a clean zero.

**Verify it is non-zero against today's tree before writing the fix** — it
should find the two Cloudflare cells.

## Scope

* The twin list gets a floor in the same style, asserted where the list is
  built rather than in one test that could be deleted with it.
* Do **not** widen the fixture product speculatively. The point is that a cell
  which pins nothing is reported, not that there are more cells.

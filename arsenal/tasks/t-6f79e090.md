Found during the test session of 2026-08-20.

The candidate said: "I can move, but in the province of Barcelona, or maximum to Girona or Tarragona.
I want to sleep home each day." None of T24's ten pinned fields can hold that:

- `location` takes a country and an onsite flag;
- `reach` takes modes (`commute`), not a distance or a region;
- `relocation` refuses `destinations` when `willingness` is `no` — correctly, since he is not relocating.

So the single most filtering thing he said survives only as quote text on an evidence row, and nothing
downstream can filter on it. `filter_hard_constraints` cannot honour a commute radius it has no field
for.

Addressed in: `src/jobsearch/candidate.py`, `src/jobsearch/constraints_step.py`, `step-02-constraints`.

Imported from issue #549

A live round reported to the candidate that **0 of 97 foreign offers publish a salary band**. That statement is false, and the way it is false is the defect: 0 were *read*. Nothing looked.

## What the connectors declare

```
tecnoempleo_es  list: company detail_url location_raw text title
                detail: company location_raw text title
talent_es       list: company detail_url location_raw title      detail: text
trabajos_es     list: company detail_url location_raw text title detail: text title
ashby_en        from_json: detail_url source_ref title location_raw text
greenhouse_en   (no salary field)
jobfluent_es    detail: ... salary_min salary_max salary_currency salary_period
```

**One connector of six can ever produce a band.** Every salary in the entire run — five of them — came from it. The candidate then reads a page saying foreign boards do not publish pay, which is a conclusion drawn from an instrument that cannot see.

## The data is there, and on Ashby it is typed

`api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/{employer}?includeCompensation=true`, measured 2026-09-22 against `baseten`: **95 of 100 postings carry `compensation.summaryComponents`**, and the salary component is selected by an explicit field value, not by position:

```json
{"compensationType": "Salary", "interval": "1 YEAR",
 "currencyCode": "USD", "minValue": 165000, "maxValue": 330000}
```

`connectors/ashby_en/connector.yaml` leaves it unmapped on purpose and says why: *"`compensation.summaryComponents` lists salary, equity and bonus in no fixed order, and this grammar cannot pick the salary one by type."* That reasoning is about the **grammar**, not the data — `from_json` has no way to say "the element whose `compensationType` is `Salary`". The same comment adds *"a missing one is visible"*, and this issue is the evidence that it is not: a missing salary is indistinguishable from a board that pays nothing, and was reported to a candidate as the latter.

Greenhouse states pay in prose inside the advert body instead: a range regex over stored text finds one in **39 of 50** greenhouse offers, none of which reached a `salary` field.

## Two changes, one grammar and one connector

- `from_json` needs a selector over a list by field value, so a typed component can be picked without depending on order.
- Boards that state pay in prose need the body parsed for a range, with the currency and period it states.

## Acceptance gate

```bash
uv run python -m integral.connector_salary_audit
```

`boards_that_publish_a_salary_we_do_not_read == 0`, measured per connector against its own committed fixture: a fixture advert whose source carries a salary must produce an offer with `salary.min` or `salary.max` set. **Counting connectors that declare a salary field is not the gate** — it is satisfiable by declaring fields that never match, which is exactly how the current shape reads as working.

Filed from a live candidate round, 2026-09-22.

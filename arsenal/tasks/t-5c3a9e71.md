Split from #688 (T251), bullet 2; filed from a candidate session (test-mode 453a5c19).

`OFFER_CEILING` is spent by whichever board is asked first, and rows count against it before the candidate's reach is applied: a worldwide board (foorilla, 195 rows) filled the run's ceiling with adverts outside the candidate's reach, 124 of 429. Since T251 the ceiling bounds the whole walk of every aim term, so a flood from one board now decides how many terms are searched at all.

## What is missing

- The ceiling is counted per board, so one board cannot spend the run's whole room.
- Rows outside the candidate's reach are not counted against it (the reach constraint is applied before the count, not after).
- The summary says which boards were capped and by how much.

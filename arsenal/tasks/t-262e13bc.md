Imported from issue #669

Found by the second reader on #666 (D-30), round 2, optional O1.

On a 30,000-triple random fuzz, 95 disagreements between `integral.robots` and `integral.second_reader` exist outside D-30's generator (same count on main and on #666's head). 94 involve a bare `%` in a rule: `robots` canonicalises it to `%25` (RFC 3986 §2.1 — a `%` not followed by two hex digits is not an escape), while `second_reader` keeps it raw so it matches the `%` of an escape in the URL — e.g. `Disallow: /*%` refuses `/x?éx`. The reviewer judges `robots` right and `second_reader` over-refusing (fail-closed).

Needs its own task: decide the reading from RFC 3986/9309, align `second_reader`, and add a measured generator for bare-`%` rules (floors = population) so the agreement count covers this class.

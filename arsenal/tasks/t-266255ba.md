The ledger's measured curl body is `{"Keyword":"python","ResultsPerPage":25}`, and `probe/captured.json` records that request. The shipped `connectors/usajobs_en/connector.yaml` sends a **third** key, `Page: "{page}"`, which no capture and no retest ever exercised.

`Pager.CurrentPageIndex` was read *out of the response*, which shows the board **reports** a page index — not that it **accepts** one in the request under that name.

It is inert today at `max_pages: 1`. It stops being inert the moment anyone raises that.

## What would settle it

One curl with `"Page":2`, checking the rows differ from page 1, recorded in `probe/`. Then either the key is measured and `max_pages` can rise, or it is dropped and `pagination.mode` goes to `none` until someone measures it.

**If the board ignores an unknown `Page` key, page 2 is a duplicate of page 1 and the fetcher collects duplicate offers** — which dedup may or may not catch, and which nothing would report.

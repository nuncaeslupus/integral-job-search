Source: Manfred Tech Career Report 2026, which gives a median hiring process of 42 days.

## Wanted

Applications marked `applied` get two time-based states, read from the application date:

- at about 21 days with no response, the candidate is reminded once that a follow-up message is reasonable (the tool never sends it);
- at about 60 days with no response, the application reads `stalled`.

`stalled` is never `rejected`: only the candidate or the employer marks a rejection. The thresholds are named constants with the report as their cited source. A response recorded at any point stops both.

## Tests

`tests/test_application_followup.py`: day 20 shows nothing, day 21 shows the reminder once, day 60 reads `stalled`; a recorded response at day 30 clears both; nothing ever reaches `rejected` by time alone.

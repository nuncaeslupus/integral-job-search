Found during the test session of 2026-08-20.

`run_checkpoint.py --id perico` for step 9 (`ranking`) printed:

    {"step": "ranking", ..., "coverage_met": true,
     "gate_metric": "explained_fraction == 1.0", "gate_owner": "T19",
     "gate_state": "not_implemented"}

and exited **0**. The script named the gate, said out loud that it does not exist, and let the
session continue. The same held for step 8 (`extraction_macro_f1`, `not_implemented`), where
`extract()` had settled 0 of 25 dimensions on every advert.

This is the inert-gate problem from `claude-arsenal/AGENTS.md` — "a gate that runs nothing passes
everything" — one level up: a *step* whose gate is unbuilt still reports the machine-visible half of
its stop rule as met. `coverage_met` answers "are the artefacts present", which a step can satisfy by
writing files nobody checked.

Decide deliberately between:
- exit non-zero on `gate_state != implemented` (a step with no gate cannot be certified), or
- keep exit 0 but require the step skill to say the gate is unbuilt before presenting its output.

What must not stand is a clean exit that reads, to a caller, as "this step passed".

Addressed in: the shared `run_checkpoint.py` (all thirteen step skills), `src/jobsearch/step_gates.py`.

Follow-up to T214 (#592, PR #659). These are the non-blocking findings from the round-2 second read.

1. **R1, fail-open, minor.** `connectors._take("period", "4000 PLN, 8 h/day")` (also `8h/day`) returns `day`.
   - A stated period skips the annual bound, so a monthly band is shown as a daily wage.
   - `_BARE_WORKING_TIME` only covers per-week phrases.
   - Fix: a closed rule that treats "a count of time units per period" as working time, not as pay.
2. **R2, test hygiene.** `test_a_non_zero_key_fails_the_audit_exit_code` calls `_main`, and `_main` calls `write_evidence`.
   - That rewrites the committed `status/evidence/T200.json` with `salary_rows_refused_for_bound_or_period: 1`.
   - Fix: point the test at `tmp_path`, or monkeypatch `write_evidence`.
3. **R3, an unsourced figure stated as measured.** The comment on `salary_recovery._UNITS_PER_EUR` cites "the ECB … reference rates, read at their level of early October 2026". The rates were actually written from memory.
   - Fix: reword the comment as approximate, or commit a dated capture of the rates.
4. **R4, loose pins.**
   - A rate wrong by ×2 in either direction stays green (SEK 5.5 or 22), because only the minimum of the band is tested.
   - `por` in the waiver word list is redundant; dropping it stays green.
   - `_WORKING_TIME` in `take: period` is likewise redundant; dropping it stays green.

Imported from issue #298

Found by the second-reader audit of #295 (T89). Not blocking that PR; recorded so the inconsistency is settled deliberately rather than discovered.

`{Filters: {inner: "{page}"}}` on its own is **refused** at load: `pagination.param` must name a *top-level* key holding the placeholder, so `_a_page_placeholder_and_a_body_field_imply_each_other` raises "carrying the page number".

Add a top-level `Page: "{page}"` beside it and the same nested value **loads and substitutes** — `_page_substituted` walks the whole structure. Measured: `{"Filters": {"inner": 2}, "ResultsPerPage": 25, "Page": 2}`.

Same shape, opposite verdict, decided by something else in the document.

## Pick one

Either nested placeholders are legal — and the lone case should load, with `pagination.param` allowed to name a path — or they are not, and the walk should substitute only the named top-level key.

Today a connector author who nests a placeholder gets a load error, adds a top-level one to satisfy it, and **silently acquires a second substitution they did not ask for**. That is the failure worth fixing: the error message teaches a workaround that changes the request.

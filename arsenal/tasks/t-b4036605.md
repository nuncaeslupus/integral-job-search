Follow-up from the second-reader report on #792 (T255), finding N6, not fixed there.

`connect_manual(..., title="Open application")` still stores the old hand-written shape (`source=manual`, an invented title, form questions in the description) as an ordinary advert, and a store that already holds such records keeps treating them as vacancies: ranked, liveness-checked, deduplicated.

Wanted: a measurement over a profile's offer store that names records of that shape, and a refusal (or a conversion to `offers.open_application`) at `connect_manual` when the input is one. The detection rule has to be stated in terms of the record, not of one title string.

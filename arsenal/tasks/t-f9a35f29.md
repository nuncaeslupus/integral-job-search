Imported from issue #548

## What happens

Step 6 elicits part-worths by conjoint and writes them to `profile/weights.json`. Nothing checks that the dimensions it prices are dimensions **adverts actually state**, so a conjoint can price two dimensions the corpus almost never carries and the L2 ranking silently degenerates.

## Measured on a live run

One candidate's weights priced exactly two dimensions. Their extraction coverage over the reachable offer set:

```
mission_alignment    1 / 111    (0.9%)
stack_modernity     26 / 111    (23%)
```

against an extraction engine that is healthy everywhere else in the same set:

```
tool_specificity        62 / 111
ai_in_the_work          42 / 111
seniority_expectation   34 / 111
```

`rank.salary_equivalent_total` returns `None` when **any** priced dimension is absent from `scores`, so it returned `None` for all 111. Every candidate then landed on the Pareto frontier, `dominates()` returned False on the first unknown axis for every pair, and the ordering fell through to **id order**. The ranking was alphabetical and nothing said so.

## Why it is invisible

Nothing in the ranking path distinguishes "these offers are genuinely incomparable" from "the priced dimensions are never stated". Both produce a full frontier and no total. A candidate is shown a list in a deliberate-looking order that carries no information.

## Two separable defects

1. **Step 6 can price an unstatable dimension.** The conjoint's candidate dimensions should be weighted toward — or at minimum reported against — measured corpus coverage, so a candidate is not asked to trade off something no advert will ever declare.
2. **A degenerate ranking presents itself as a ranking.** `rank()` knows it produced no totals and that every candidate is on the frontier; that is a reportable state, not an ordering.

The second is the one that reaches the candidate, and it is cheap.

## Acceptance gate

```bash
uv run python -m integral.rank --degeneracy-evidence
```

`rankings_presented_without_a_single_total == 0`. The corpus-coverage half belongs with step 6 and needs its own measurement — coverage of each priced dimension over `corpus/labelled/ads.jsonl`, reported at elicitation time rather than discovered at ranking time.

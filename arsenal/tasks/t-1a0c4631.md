Imported from issue #931

**Severity:** medium · **Confidence:** medium · **Category:** error-handling

**Where:** `src/integral/identity.py:227-300, src/integral/lifecycle.py:283-290, src/integral/offers.py:465-499`

### Finding
`ProfileStore.write_text` opens the target with O_TRUNC and then writes, so a Ctrl-C, crash or full disk between the two leaves an empty or half-written JSON for constraints, CV store, offers or lifecycle records; readers then raise IdentityError or (for offers) silently skip the record into `_unreadable.jsonl`.

### Evidence
Traced: _open_leaf mode 'w' -> os.open(O_WRONLY|O_CREAT|O_TRUNC|O_NOFOLLOW), no temp file, no fsync/replace anywhere (`grep -n 'os.replace' src/integral/identity.py` empty). Not reproduced by killing a process.

### Suggested direction
Write to a sibling temp file opened with O_EXCL|O_NOFOLLOW and `os.replace` it over the target.

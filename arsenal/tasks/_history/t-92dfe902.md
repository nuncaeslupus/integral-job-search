Review leftovers from #965 (publisher kind), batched per policy G after its second review round. Full cases are in the two second-reader reports on #965.

Fail-open in `integral.publisher_kind`. Today the impact is nil, because no step writes a `publisher:` exclusion yet; it becomes real once one does:

1. English "client" used as a modifier reads as a placement: "We are looking for a client-facing Solutions Engineer", "You will join a client-facing team", "Join our Client Success team!", "Work with our client success managers", "We are hiring for our Client Services team." These return `consultancy`, while the same sentences in ES and CA return `unknown`, so parity breaks too. Cause: every EN pattern ends in `client\b`, which also matches before a hyphen or a following noun.
2. Customer-first slogans and support wording in the singular: "Our client is at the heart of everything", "Nuestro cliente es lo primero", "darás soporte a un cliente", "built X for a client in 2019".
3. `Exclusion.value` is not validated for the `publisher` facet: `publisher:consultora` silently excludes nothing, and `publisher:unknown` excludes every advert with no cue. Only `consultancy` and `agency` may be recorded.

Fail-closed:

4. A negator in an earlier comma clause cancels a real cue: "No es necesario inglés, trabajarás en cliente final" returns `unknown`.
5. A self-description is missed: "Somos una consultora" / "We are a consultancy" / "Som una consultora".

## Wanted

Close item 1 with one rule (a cue matches only when "client" is the head noun, not a modifier or the first half of a hyphenated word), applied to every language row. Every case above becomes a test in ES, EN and CA. The recorder refuses a `publisher` value outside {consultancy, agency}.

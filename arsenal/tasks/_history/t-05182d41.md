Found on 2026-09-11 running step 8 over real employer-ATS adverts (English), and
both faults reached the **candidate's offer card**: a healthcare benefit was
printed as the employer's mission and priced into the salary-equivalent total.

Two root causes, one shape — a cue reported words the advert did not say.

## 1. Every cue matched as a substring, and ten readers each matched their own way

`re.finditer(cue.pattern, text, re.IGNORECASE)` was written out at ten call
sites — the rules stage, the negation audit, the gold check, the silent-slice
check, the cue audit, the prefilter's span check, the harness, suggestions,
elicitation and two tests. Each read a cue as a substring, so, measured over the
committed corpus:

| cue | fired inside | dimension it scored |
|---|---|---|
| `go(lang)?\b` | Django, Chicago, Argo, "lingo" | `stack_modernity` +0.6 |
| `rust\b` | high-trust, trusted, frustration, Zero Trust | `stack_modernity` +0.6 |
| `ret[ée]n` | retención, Retention, entretenimiento | `on_call_load` 0.8 |
| `maternity cover` | Maternity Coverage (an insurance benefit) | `contract_stability` 0.3 |
| `legado` | "Llegados a este punto" | `stack_modernity` −0.7 |
| `aut[óo]nomo` | "despliegues autónomos" | `contract_stability` 0.0 |
| `conciliaci[óo]n` | "Conciliaciones bancarias" | `work_intensity` −0.6 |
| `our mission is` | "**Y**our mission is to…" | `mission_alignment` |
| `our product` | "y**our product** area" | `product_vs_services` |
| `mentor` | "cual dementor" | `mentoring_culture` 0.8 |

## 2. `mission_alignment` scored a sector noun as a purpose

Its own definition rules that out — *"a named purpose, rather than a sector left
to infer"* — and a regex cannot tell `healthcare` the purpose from `Healthcare`
the benefit, `Educació Infantil` the degree, `Administración Pública` the client,
`L'Hospitalet` the town, or `sanitaris` the bathroom fittings. Over thirty corpus
adverts settled 0.7 that way. The cue also carried 0.6, which is not one of this
dimension's two rungs (0.0 and 0.7).

## Design

- **One guard, in the model.** `Cue.finditer` / `Cue.search` wrap the pattern in
  a whole-word assertion; every reader routes through them. **Letters**, not
  `\w`: scraped adverts glue numbers to words ("Modalidad100 remoto"), and a
  digit beside a letter is not a word split in two. The assertion is inside the
  regex, so the engine backtracks past a split rather than rejecting the match
  (`(cliente|clientes)` over "clientes" yields the longer alternative).
- **A stem says it is one** — `escalab\w*`, `sprints?`, `contrataci[óo]n
  indefinid\w*`. Four of these are invisible to the corpus and were found by
  taking each pattern's final literal and asking whether it ever occurs as a
  whole word in that language's adverts.
- **An ambiguous proper noun scopes case-folding off and needs a frame.**
  `(?-i:Go)` counts only as an item of a delimited list, never before a hyphen.
  Case alone cannot do it: a heading capitalises the verb.
- **A sector counts where the organisation's own mission statement names it**,
  and never in a second-person clause — a benefit is what the employer does for
  *you*; a purpose is for somebody else. Everything else goes unsettled, which
  is what sends it to the model.

  file — an AST scan: a reader that calls `re` on `cue.pattern` gets the old
  substring match back, silently, which is how ten of them did.

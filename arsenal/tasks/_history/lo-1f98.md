## What this is

Step 12 of the process (`status/spec-v2-process.md` §2). The candidate comes
back from an interview; the tool records what was asked and what happened, and
turns it into preparation for the next one.

The step exists in the settled step list and its gate metric was named there
with no owning task — this is that task.

## The gate

`interview_lesson_linkage` = the fraction of logged interviews producing at
least one evidence row linked to a dimension or a story-bank episode. It must
be 1.0.

An interview that produces only prose is a diary entry. The point of logging it
is that the next interview goes better and the profile learns something — "they
pushed hard on on-call and I had no answer" is evidence about `on_call_load`
*and* a gap in the story bank, and it must land in `evidence.jsonl` as both or
the feedback loop is decorative.

## Retention

`interviews/<offer_id>/` is **historical** (`spec-v2-process.md` §3.4):
immutable, never revised, and never purged — an offer with an interview record
is exempt from the 60-day purge regardless of its status.

## Tests

`test_every_logged_interview_produces_linked_evidence`;
`test_an_interview_record_is_never_purged_with_its_offer`;
`test_preparation_draws_only_from_the_story_bank` — the same
invent-nothing rule the generated CV is held to (S4).

## Scope change — v2 plan, 2026-08-18

**Narrowed.** S6 is preparation and the log: what this employer is likely to
press on, drawn from the advert's emphases, what the application claimed and
what previous interviews asked; then afterwards the questions asked, the
outcome, the lessons — `interviews/<offer_id>/`, immutable and exempt from purge
regardless of the offer's status. It owns step 12's gate,
`interview_lesson_linkage == 1.0`.

**The strict mock role-play splits out as T47**, because its rule — announced
first, no coaching mid-answer, no breaking character, feedback only at the end —
is a different discipline with its own gate, and it is the one place in the tool
where the warm register is wrong.

Ask about a real interview gently and soon, and not at all if they clearly do
not want to relive it. A bad interview is a bad day.

## What was built — 2026-08-23

`src/integral/interview_log.py`, measured over every advert in the labelled
corpus: **96 interviews logged, 0 unlinked, `interview_lesson_linkage` 1.0**,
with 4 adverts refused for teaching nothing.

**Linkage is structural, not procedural** — the same choice T45 made about
claims. `log_interview` refuses a set of lessons that names neither a dimension
nor a story episode, so a linkless record cannot be written; the measurement
then reads the **records on disk** against the **log on disk**, so it still sees
an interview however it got there.
`test_the_linkage_check_would_notice_a_record_written_by_hand` plants one and
requires the fraction to drop to 0.5 and name it.

**A cited episode lends its dimensions.** A lesson links either way — *"they
asked about autonomy"* names a dimension, *"that story landed badly"* names an
episode — and the second resolves through the log to the dimensions T8 linked
that episode to. One rule covers both, and the row carries real dimension ids
either way.

**The 4 refusals are the honest branch.** Those adverts fire no ad-side cue, so
nothing is pressed on, so no lesson links. They are counted in the evidence
rather than dropped: a gate reported over only the interviews that happened to
link would be a gate met by discarding the awkward ones.

**The record is historical, the preparation is authored** — `held.json` and
`outcome.json` are both `open("x")`, which is the whole of immutability, and
that is what makes "the outcome came four days later" an append rather than a
revision. `revision.py`'s `_CLASSES` already keyed `interviews/*/preparation`
that way; the layout here is chosen to match it, and
`test_preparation_is_authored_and_the_record_is_historical` asserts it through
`classify` rather than describing it.

`EvidenceSubject` gained `kind="interview"` with its own id shape
(`<offer_id>/iv-<N>`) — what the D-8 docstring said a future kind should do when
it landed.

Step 12's gate state moves `not_implemented` → `implemented` across the five
places D-7's ownership check enforces.

Two coupled defects, found while building T144 (#445).

**1. No `country: GLOBAL` package is ever selected.** `sourcing.packages_for`
keeps a package only when its `country` equals the candidate's, and a
candidate's country must match `^[A-Z]{2}$`. So foorilla, arbeitnow, himalayas,
pythonorg, landingjobs, weworkremotely, wellfound, remotive, workingnomads — and
T144's five ATS hosts — are installed and never consulted. No test mentions
GLOBAL.

**2. Volume.** A board without `{query}` returns its whole list and every row
becomes an offer. Greenhouse alone lists ~6,500 postings per run. Step 7's stop
rule requires *"a per-run offer ceiling"* and there is none. Rows past
`DETAIL_FETCH_CEILING` are counted as `dropped` with the build error ("no text")
as their reason, which misreports a spent budget as a broken advert.

Fixed together, so that turning GLOBAL selection on cannot flood a tree.

## Decisions (owner, 2026-09-10)

- **GLOBAL is selected when `reach` is stated and includes `remote` or
  `cross_border_remote_employer`**, after the candidate's own country's
  packages. Unknown or declined reach selects none, for the reason
  `packages_for` already gives for an unknown location, and the summary says so.
- **`OFFER_CEILING = 50`** new offers per run, across all boards. Once reached,
  no further detail fetch, no further `collect_offer`, no further board; what
  was stopped is reported.
- **An unsteered board with no aim terms is skipped**, exactly like a steered
  one: "say what you are looking for".

## Design

An unsteered board's rows are matched against the aim phrases **before** any
detail fetch and before `collect_offer`. A row matches a phrase when every word
of the phrase appears as a whole word in the row's `title` plus any list-row
`text`, casefolded and accent-folded, in any order — the AND a board applies to
a query. This is the same match a `{query}` board performs on its own server,
which is why it does not fall under `Aim`'s objection to a title filter: rows it
removes are counted (`BoardOutcome.off_aim`) and printed, never silent. No word
list, so ES/EN/CA behave identically.

Not done: filtering rows by constraint (`location_raw`) before the detail fetch
— a free-text parser that fails closed, and step 8/9's hard filter owns it.

## The defect

A live candidate session on 2026-09-10 sourced for a pharmacy technician /
dermoconsejera in Barcelona with four phrases: `técnico farmacia`,
`dermocosmética`, `parafarmacia`, `farmacia online atención al cliente`. Of 81
offers returned by the six ES boards, **none** was a pharmacy, parapharmacy or
dermocosmetics job. The installed ES boards are tech-heavy. The one general
board, `trabajos_es`, pointed at the fixed `atencion_al_cliente` category, so
it handed every candidate the same forty customer-service adverts whatever
they had asked for.

The gap is **sector**, not language (compare the DE/FR/IT/NL/PT finding in
`connectors/ruled-out.yaml`'s `language_unsupported`).

## The fix

trabajos.com has a keyword search, `?CADENA=`, and it answers correctly.
Measured on 2026-09-10:

- `técnico farmacia` returns 40 rows, 21 of them pharmacy, lab or cosmetics
  titles and 11 in Barcelona;
- `python` returns developer roles;
- a nonsense word returns **zero** rows rather than a fallback list, so an
  empty answer means empty.

`url_pattern` becomes `https://www.trabajos.com/ofertas-empleo/?CADENA={query}`.
The search page serves the same `.listado2014.card.oferta` markup as the
category page the committed fixture was recorded from. A live run of the
changed package, over the candidate's four phrases, gave 49 offers added, 0
dropped, and 8 of 8 detail fetches succeeding.

## The survey, recorded

The same session surveyed the pharmacy-sector boards the connector-new skill
names and recorded each in `connectors/ruled-out.yaml` with a retest. Three
boards are readable and each needs an engine change, which the skill says to
file rather than work around in a connector file:

| board | gap |
|---|---|
| farmatalent.com | the advert href is on the list item itself, and field selectors read only descendants |
| infoempleo.com | a query with no hits returns ten fallback adverts in the same `li.offerblock`; a simple selector cannot exclude `.ShortResult` |
| pmfarma.com | the body is only in `api.pmfarma.com/api/oferta/{id}`, and that fetch URL outranks a mapped `url` in `build_offer` |

The rest are out:

- The COFB board needs a login and robots-refuses `/group/guest/`.
- The CGCOF portal is rendered through admin-ajax and lists no Barcelona.
- es.jooble.org and es.indeed.com answer 403.
- opcionempleo.com serves a challenge page, which was not bypassed.
- The robots.txt of jobatus.es and laboris.net cannot be read.

None of the engine changes is in this task. Each touches `connectors.py` or
`sourcing.py`, where T144 is currently changing the list format, so they wait
for it.

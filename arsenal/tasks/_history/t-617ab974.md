The owner ruled on this mid-session, and again on 2026-09-01. Both halves matter
and they point in different directions.

**No candidate is ever served from the corpus.**

> About the local corpus, I think it's not a good idea to use it for real
> candidates. Each candidate must have their own ads searched at the moment for
> them. […] the idea is to look for relevant ads ALWAYS, especially when the
> process is advancing and more specific ads are needed.

An advert is perishable and a stored one is stale by definition. Using the
corpus as a source of offers is what let the live session return three adverts
and call the market exhausted.

**And the corpus must not be built from one candidate's harvest either.** The
live session harvested ~2,755 adverts. Landing them is tempting — five open
tasks sit behind D-23 — and it is the wrong sample. Those rows are the shape of
*one person's* queries and exclusions. A gate measured on them reports accuracy
for that person, and the number would be quoted as accuracy for everyone. The
owner's framing on 2026-09-01: this must stay a **generic tool**.

**What replaces it.** The connector library is the answer to its own problem: a
corpus can now be drawn by specification — job families, languages, regions —
with no candidate in the loop, and **redrawn**, which a one-off session harvest
never can be. D-23 needs adverts labelled for permit, citizenship, clearance and
role-language; a deliberate draw can be enriched for exactly those, where the
harvest happens to contain whatever it contains. (One measurement from the
session argues the enrichment is real: 117 of 213 USD adverts carrying a band
were US/Canada-restricted.)

This task writes the rule and the draw. It does not label — that is D-23.

**The one exemption, decided by the owner on 2026-09-04.** Step 5
(`integral.reaction_elicit`) draws corpus adverts as **stimuli** — adverts shown to the
candidate so a reaction can be captured — and that reads the corpus. It is **exempt**,
because *"reacting to an advert can never contaminate the labels it is scored
against"*: showing an advert to elicit a reaction is measurement, not a search result,
so the perishability argument that bans serving does not reach it.

The exemption came with a condition and it is the load-bearing half: **the stimuli must
come from a split disjoint from the evaluation set.** An advert a candidate has reacted
to is no longer clean held-out data for anything scored on the evaluation split.

So `reaction_elicit` does not join `CORE_SERVING_MODULES`, and three bounds are
measured rather than promised — an unbounded exemption is a hole with a comment next to
it. It may import `integral.offers` and `integral.lifecycle` and nothing else on the
serving path; a serving module importing *it* is a corpus read like any direct one; and
no advert reachable as a stimulus may carry an evaluation-split advert's text.

## Acceptance gate

```gate
corpus_measurement_set_violations == 0
evidence: status/evidence/T98.json
key: corpus_measurement_set_violations
status-key: gate_status
```

`corpus_measurement_set_violations` is one key because `gate_evidence.py` asserts one,
and it is the sum of **five**, each reported beside it under its own name — a sum whose
components are not named is a zero that hides what it is made of:

| component | what it counts |
|---|---|
| `corpus_rows_without_a_draw_specification` | rows no declared draw accounts for |
| `serving_path_corpus_reads` | serving-path modules reaching the corpus, through the exempt reader included |
| `exempt_reader_serving_imports` | the exempt reader reaching the serving path |
| `stimulus_pool_evaluation_overlaps` | adverts reachable as stimuli that the evaluation split also holds |
| `corpus_text_collisions` | one advert's text under two corpus ids — how an overlap arrives after a re-split |

The block used to declare the first alone, which left the serving ban — the half the
task is named after — outside the gate: a serving module reading the corpus kept
`corpus_rows_without_a_draw_specification` at zero and `gate_status` at `measured`, so
T98's own gate passed the regression T98 exists to prevent.

Four denominators are committed, all as **floors** (T100) rather than counts of the
day: `corpus_rows_evaluated_at_least`, `serving_path_modules_scanned_at_least`,
`stimulus_reachable_adverts_at_least` — the exemption's reach, as a measured quantity
rather than a claim — and `evaluation_adverts_compared_at_least`. The last two are a
pair because the disjointness reading has **two** vacuous passes: an empty stimulus pool
overlaps nothing, and an empty evaluation split is overlapped by nothing. A breach of
any floor makes the reading `unmeasured`, not a clean zero.

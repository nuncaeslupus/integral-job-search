The owner ruled on this mid-session, and again on 2026-09-01. Both halves matter
and they point in different directions.

**No candidate is ever served from the corpus.**

> About the local corpus, I think it's not a good idea to use it for real
> candidates. Each candidate must have their own ads searched at the moment for
> them. […] the idea is to look for relevant ads ALWAYS, especially when the
> process is advancing and more specific ads are needed.

An advert is perishable and a stored one is stale by definition. Using the
corpus as a source of offers is what let the live session return three adverts
and call the market exhausted.

**And the corpus must not be built from one candidate's harvest either.** The
live session harvested ~2,755 adverts. Landing them is tempting — five open
tasks sit behind D-23 — and it is the wrong sample. Those rows are the shape of
*one person's* queries and exclusions. A gate measured on them reports accuracy
for that person, and the number would be quoted as accuracy for everyone. The
owner's framing on 2026-09-01: this must stay a **generic tool**.

**What replaces it.** The connector library is the answer to its own problem: a
corpus can now be drawn by specification — job families, languages, regions —
with no candidate in the loop, and **redrawn**, which a one-off session harvest
never can be. D-23 needs adverts labelled for permit, citizenship, clearance and
role-language; a deliberate draw can be enriched for exactly those, where the
harvest happens to contain whatever it contains. (One measurement from the
session argues the enrichment is real: 117 of 213 USD adverts carrying a band
were US/Canada-restricted.)

This task writes the rule and the draw. It does not label — that is D-23.

## Acceptance gate

```gate
corpus_measurement_set_violations == 0
evidence: status/evidence/T98.json
key: corpus_measurement_set_violations
status-key: gate_status
```

`corpus_measurement_set_violations` is
`corpus_rows_without_a_draw_specification + serving_path_corpus_reads`, and it is one
key because `gate_evidence.py` asserts one. The block used to declare the first alone,
which left the serving ban — the half the task is named after — outside the gate: a
serving module reading the corpus kept `corpus_rows_without_a_draw_specification` at
zero and `gate_status` at `measured`, so T98's own gate passed the regression T98
exists to prevent.

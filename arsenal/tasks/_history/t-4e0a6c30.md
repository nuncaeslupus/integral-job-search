## The defect

Verified 2026-09-10 on `main` at 16dc93b. `lifecycle.collect_offer` — the one
gate every connector, the sourcing loop and `reaction_elicit.collect_stimuli`
route through — only consults **tombstones**. For an offer already stored in
`offers/<id>.json` and not tombstoned, it falls through to `track_new_offer` +
`save_lifecycle_offer`, which overwrites the offer body and its lifecycle record
with a fresh one-event `status: new` history, and returns `added_as_new=True`.

Reproduction: `collect_offer` an offer, `transition` it to `shortlisted` and
save it, `collect_offer` the same offer again → `added_as_new=True` and
`load_lifecycle_offer(...)[0].status == "new"`.

So the candidate's shortlist is silently lost on the next sourcing run. It also
restarts §7.3's purge clock (`collected_at` is re-stamped), and
`sourcing.BoardOutcome.added` / `Run.summary()` count every re-sighting as a
newly added offer while `BoardOutcome`'s docstring says `added` is what survived
dedup.

## What the metric counts

`live_offers_reset_by_recollection`, in `status/evidence/S5.json`: S5's probe
re-collects **every live offer in its store** the way a connector would
(`connect_manual` over the stored text and url, so `status: new`), then counts
the offers whose on-disk body or lifecycle record differs afterwards, or whose
outcome claimed `added_as_new`. Read off disk, not off what the call returned
alone. The re-collections are `scenarios_checked` members, so `MINIMUM_SCENARIOS`
floors the population.

## Scope

* Fixed in `collect_offer`, not in its callers.
* Near-duplicates of a **live** offer (a different id normalising to the same
  text) are dedup's question, not this one's.

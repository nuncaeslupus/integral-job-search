## The defect

Found by the second reader on #447 (T166). A connector whose
`list.url_pattern` carries `{query}` (`connectors.accepts_query`) is only
proven to *send* the candidate's terms, never that the board reads them.
Swapping `trabajos_es`'s `?CADENA={query}` for `?q={query}` — a key
trabajos.com ignores, answering with forty unrelated adverts — passed every
test and `make evidence`. T166 closed it for `trabajos_es` alone, with a test
rebuilding the live probe's URL from the connector.

`jobfluent_es` is a live instance on `main` today: its `url_pattern` is
`…/empleos-barcelona?q={query}&page={page}` while `probe/captured.json`
records `…/empleos-barcelona?page=2`. No capture ever carried `q`.

## The rule

A closed rule over every package, in `integral.pagination_capture`'s idiom
(T113 certifies that every pagination key a package sends appears in a
recorded capture): for every package where `accepts_query` is true, the
captured URL must be a URL the connector could issue, with every `{query}`
position — query-string key or path segment — **filled**. A key the board was
never asked with cannot pass. Violations are reported by package name; the
packages checked are a denominator committed as a literal floor (T100/T122).

`jobfluent_es`'s probe is re-recorded live from a `?q=<term>&page=2` search,
with robots adjudicated by `integral.robots`, the board shown to honour `q` (a
nonsense term changes the rows), and provenance `live` with the response's
`{file, bytes, sha256}` per `integral.capture_provenance`.

> I see you had some problems with reading the CV or knowing what to do with
> it. Take note to fix it for next times.

Step 1 is where everything downstream gets its facts. It stumbled, and the
session continued as though it had not — so every later step ran on a thinner
profile than the candidate had actually supplied, and nothing said so.

Two failures are worth separating, because the note names both and they need
different fixes: *reading* it (the file arriving as text at all — `ats.py` owns
the text-layer contract) and *knowing what to do with it* (which fields the
document is expected to yield).

The behaviour to fix is the silence. A partial read is acceptable; a partial
read that reports success is what leaves the candidate answering questions the
CV already answered.

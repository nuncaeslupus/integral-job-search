Filed from a candidate session (test-mode 658fcce2). The owner's instruction for this round: the
project does a hard job to be able to sort offers, and anything that keeps it from sorting
correctly at this point is a design flaw.

## What happened

"Can I do this without much trouble" was half of the candidate's question. The project measures
pieces of it and adds none to the order:

- `stack_fit` is computed per offer and is documented as stated-not-scored: it moves nothing.
- `seniority_expectation` is extracted from some adverts (a Staff and a Senior role among the
  eleven) and never compared with the level the candidate last held.
- `english_demand` is extracted where an advert states it and never compared with the candidate's
  own spoken and written levels, which the profile records separately.

So a Staff role asking for five missing technologies ranks exactly as a mid role whose stack the
candidate holds.

## What is missing

- A fit reading per offer that combines stack, seniority gap and language demand against the
  candidate, carried into the ranking as its own axis or as a priced dimension.
- Unknown stays unknown: an advert silent on seniority is not a match.
- A gate over constructed pairs that differ only in fit: the better-fitting offer never ranks below
  the other with everything else equal.

Filed 2026-09-30 (#597). `liveness.dead_phrase_in` matched `DEAD_PHRASES` anywhere in
the page, and every Jobfluent advert carries a site-wide report button
`<a …href="/es/offers/<id>/report-filled">Oferta no disponible? Dínoslo!</a>`, so
`read_response` returned `dead` for live adverts and `expire` retired them — fail-closed.
Measured 2026-09-30: 15 of 15 freshly sourced Jobfluent adverts read dead; with that
anchor removed, 12 read live and 3 unverified.

**The rule.** A closure notice is a statement the page makes. The text of a control
(`<a>`, `<button>`) and a question (a sentence ending in `?`) are not statements, so
neither counts. A closure stated in the page's own text beside the button still reads
dead (the fail-open guard).

## What the spec requires

`status/specification.md` §5.1 describes one dimension model as the shared
vocabulary across every stage. `status/plan.md` (Scope extension, 2026-08-16 §2)
records that this implicitly assumes every dimension is *matched* — the ad
describes an environment, the candidate holds a preference, ranking compares them.

## What the code does

`Dimension` in `src/jobsearch/dimensions.py` has no notion of side, and
`extractor_coverage` counts any dimension without cues as uncovered. So:

- a **candidate trait** (creativity, ambition, learning orientation) has no ad
  cue that could ever exist, and would sit in the model as a permanently broken
  matched dimension dragging `dimension_extractor_coverage` down;
- a **candidate fact** (languages, location, relocation, salary floor) is
  compared against an ad *requirement*, not a preference, and has no place to
  say so.

## The fix

1. Add `side: matched | candidate_fact | candidate_trait` (default `matched`,
   so the 22 committed dimensions are unchanged and the field is additive).
2. Require cues only for `matched` and, where the ad states a requirement, for
   the ad-side half a `candidate_fact` compares against.
3. `dimension_extractor_coverage` counts only ad-side dimensions; the evidence
   reports the per-side breakdown so a model that is all traits and no cues
   cannot report full coverage.
4. `side_coverage_violations` counts dimensions whose side and content disagree:
   a `matched` dimension with no cues, a `candidate_trait` carrying cues (it
   claims to be extractable from an ad and is not), a `candidate_fact` with no
   comparison target.

## Tests

Write these RED before any production code:

`test_a_trait_dimension_without_cues_is_valid` in `tests/test_dimension_side.py`
— a `candidate_trait` needs no cues and no gold; `test_a_trait_dimension_carrying_cues_is_rejected`
— claiming ad-extractability for a trait is a contradiction, not a warning;
`test_extractor_coverage_counts_only_ad_side_dimensions` — adding traits does
not depress `dimension_extractor_coverage`.

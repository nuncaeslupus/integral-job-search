Measured on 2026-09-06 over 251 live offers in a candidate's tree: two of thirteen installed sources publish a salary band at all (himalayas 20/28, justjoin 15/24). Tecnoempleo 0/49, weworkremotely 0/33, workingnomads 0/30, builtin 0/20, ticjob 0/20, trabajos 0/15, pythonorg 0/10, infojobs 0/3.

A candidate whose stated rule is 'if the salary is not published and cannot be approximated, do not show me the offer' therefore sees eight offers out of 251 — not because the market is thin but because the boards we read do not publish pay.

`ai-jobs.net` is AI/ML-specific, remote-heavy, and publishes a band on most listings. Build it with T32's declarative format, `connectors/examplejobs_es/` as the worked example.

## Scope

- robots.txt read in full and quoted in the connector header, per the house style every existing connector follows
- list + detail selectors, `salary` mapped
- fixture and probe captures, distinct offers on each side (T127: a probe that is a re-capture of the fixture measures nothing)
- `meta.yaml` declaring market and locale


## Outcome

No new package. `ai-jobs.net` and `aijobs.net` (and their `/robots.txt`) both answer 301 to
`https://foorilla.com/hiring/data-ai-and-machine-learning/` (measured 2026-10-05), and
`connectors/foorilla_en/` (T134) already reads that board, with `salary_min`, `salary_max` and
`salary_currency` mapped from the advert page's band. A second package for the same host would
duplicate every offer. Adjudicated with the repo matcher on 2026-10-05: `foorilla.com/hiring/jobs/?job_search=machine+learning`
True; negative controls `/hiring/companies/x` False and `/hiring/jobs/x/apply/` False.

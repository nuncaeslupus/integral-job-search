Found during the test session of 2026-08-20 (candidate: a Barcelona construction worker).

`extraction.extract()` settled **0 of 25 dimensions on all seven adverts**. `dimensions/` is entirely
software-sector: `stack_modernity`, `on_call_load`, `english_demand`, `product_vs_services`,
`technical_depth`. For a bricklayer the whole of step 8 is inert, so step 9 ranked by hand — which the
skill explicitly forbids ("never a paragraph assembled by a model").

This is not a bug in `extract`; the vocabulary does not cover the market. Decide deliberately:
a sector-scoped dimension set, or an honest refusal when no dimension applies. What must not stand is
step 8 reporting success over a vocabulary that matched nothing.

Addressed in: `dimensions/`, `src/integral/extraction.py`, `step-08-understanding`.

## The decision — honest refusal, not a sector-scoped model

Of the two resolutions this task put up, widening the model per sector was
already retired: the owner's 2026-08-19 scope change settled that dimensions are
coined when a live session turns one up, not by a corpus sweep. So the answer is
the refusal, and `step_gates.VOCABULARY_SILENT` (exit 4) carries it.

**It gets its own exit code deliberately.** Reusing D-21's `UNCERTIFIABLE` would
make "the gate nobody built" indistinguishable from "the gate is fine and the
words do not fit this trade", and those are owed by different people — one by a
task, one by the dimension model.

**And it must not depend on the gate.** Step 8's acceptance gate
(`extraction_macro_f1 >= 0.75`, owned by T56) is `not_implemented`, so D-21
already keeps that checkpoint off 0 — by accident, for an unrelated reason. The
day T56 builds that gate the masking disappears and a silent vocabulary would
certify. `the_refusal_does_not_depend_on_the_gate` is that claim, asserted.

Presence and reach stay separate, for D-21's own reason: an extraction that
settled nothing is not a missing artefact — the step ran and produced exactly
what it had to say — so reporting it absent would read as "still working"
rather than "this vocabulary does not cover this market".

T113 added a `provenance` field to `probe/captured.json` so that "transcribed
from a committed curl" and "recorded live" are distinguishable in the artefact
rather than in a pull request comment. It is **advisory only**: 19 of 20
captures read `unrecorded`, nothing fails on any value, and a fabricated
capture claiming `live` passes.

That is the field's whole subject turned on itself. A provenance marker nobody
checks records an intention, not a fact, and the defect T113 exists to refuse —
an artefact certifying a request nobody made — is exactly what an unchecked
`live` would wave through.

Either the value is checked against something (a committed curl for
`transcribed`, a recorded timestamp and response shape for `live`), or the
field is honest about being a label and the gate says so. What must not persist
is a marker that reads as evidence and is not.

Filed from the second-reader report on #406, which named it rather than
widening that diff.

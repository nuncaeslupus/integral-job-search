From a test-mode note captured 2026-09-06 at step `sourcing`:

> By the way, we must understand any user will prefer a job that pays more between two similar jobs. That should be a rule.

It is not one today. Pay reaches the ranking as one signal among the others, so two offers alike in every respect except salary can come back in either order, and nothing catches it.

The rule is a **dominance** rule, not a weight: where two offers are equal on every other ranked dimension, the higher-paying one is never ranked below the lower-paying one. Stated that way it is checkable, and it does not require deciding how much pay is worth against a commute or a stack — which is a preference and belongs where preferences live.

Two cases have to be decided explicitly rather than falling out of the arithmetic, and the task is not done until the answer is written down:

- **One of the two publishes no band.** An unpublished salary is not a low one. Today 97 of 561 offers carry a band, so this is the common case, not the edge — and treating a missing number as zero would bury every offer from a board that does not publish.
- **Different currencies, or a range against a point.** Comparing `€50.000–65.000` with `$70.000` is a conversion and a choice of which end of the range to compare; both must be stated, not implied.

`take: "range_low"` / `"range_high"` / `"currency"` (T133) is what makes the comparison possible at all — before it, a range was one unparsed string.

A violation is a ranked pair, equal on every other ranked dimension, in which
the lower-paying offer is placed above the higher-paying one. Pairs are built
by holding one offer and varying **only** the salary, so a violation cannot be
argued away as some other signal having moved.

The two decided cases carry their own rows and their own count: a pair where
one side publishes no band, and a pair whose bands are in different currencies
or where a range meets a point. Neither may resolve by treating an absent
number as zero — 97 of 561 offers measured on 2026-09-06 carried a band at
all, so that reading would bury every board that does not publish.

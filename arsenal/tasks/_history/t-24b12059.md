The two boards the 2026-09-06 survey chose, built on T130, T132 and T133.

**foorilla.com** — 259,753 adverts in 60 days, remote-heavy, a real `?job_search=` parameter, and the destination of both `ai-jobs.net` and `aijobs.net`. First connector to declare `client: htmx`; body and salary come from the advert's own page, so it is also the first to depend on T130.

**landing.jobs** — European, hires into the EU as a matter of course, and the third of sixteen sources to publish a salary band at all — the only one writing it in euros. `?q=` narrows 50 cards to 15.

## The two `take:` gaps they found

- `CAD 150K-190K` — the `K` is a **multiplier**. Read as 150 to 190 it is a 1000x error that looks entirely reasonable in a ranking, which is the failure `_as_float` already refuses by another door. `K` and `M` are now read, and only immediately after the figure: `40k hires, €50.000 - €65.000` finds three numbers and returns nothing.
- `CAD` and `AUD` added to the currency table.

Five new contracts, floor raised 11 → 16.

## Two committed counts moved, and both were checked rather than bumped

- `get_connectors_still_plain_gets` compared every GET connector's headers against the **empty dict**. foorilla is the first package that legitimately carries some. Relaxing it to "headers are allowed now" would retire the check; it compares against `client_headers(...)` **derived from the package's own declaration** instead, so a package acquiring a header nobody declared is still caught.
- `robots_adjudications_without_a_competent_second_reader` 19 → 20. foorilla's second reader ran and could not refuse — its robots.txt opens with `Allow: /`, and CPython returns the first matching rule rather than RFC 9309's longest match, so it answered True to all four paths including the two the repo matcher refuses. That is this ledger's documented failure mode met on a live board, and the row says so rather than claiming an agreement.


## Acceptance gate

```gate
connectors_whose_probe_repeats_its_fixture == 0
evidence: status/evidence/T127.json
key: connectors_whose_probe_repeats_its_fixture
```

The library-wide rot check, which both new packages must satisfy: fixture and
probe captured separately and naming **different** offers. That is the check
two of eighteen packages silently failed until T127, and the reason a new
package earns it rather than inheriting it.

- `foorilla_en` — fixture `?job_search=agentic` (50 rows), probe
  `?job_search=python` (50 rows), **0 shared**.
- `landingjobs_en` — fixture `?q=python` (15 rows), probe
  `?q=artificial intelligence` (4 rows), 2 shared of 4 — two new offers, and
  the boundary is one new offer, not a percentage.

`partial_extraction_contracts_failing` stays 0 over a table raised from 11 to
16 by the five cases these two boards produced, so the `K`/`M` multiplier is
covered by the gate T132 already committed rather than by a second one.

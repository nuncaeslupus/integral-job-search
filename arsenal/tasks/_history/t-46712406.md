Filed from a candidate session (test-mode 453a5c19). The owner's act-now note: fix everything said in the session.

## What happened

Two offers were given to the candidate with links that opened the board's application form (`job-boards.eu.greenhouse.io`, page title "Job Application for…") rather than the advert on the employer's careers site. He reported it; the URLs were replaced by hand.

## What is missing

- An offer's `url` is the page that describes the job. A page whose main content is an application form is not presented as the advert; the employer's own advert URL is preferred when the board API gives one.

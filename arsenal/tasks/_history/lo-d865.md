## What this is

The owner reviewed `status/spec-v2-process.md` (merged in #15) and returned 16
annotations, saved at `docs/spec-v2/notes.json`. This task folds them into the
document. Three questions they raised were answered on 2026-08-17:

- **Identification** — a short handle the candidate chooses, confirmed by
  display name at every later session. Not a legal name.
- **Cross-user deletion** — permitted, but only after confirming the target
  profile by name.
- **Required steps** — Identify, Constraints, Sourcing, Understanding, Ranking.
  Every other step is offered, improves the result, and may be declined or
  deferred without blocking.

## The changes the notes require

1. **The non-insistence rule** (§5.4). Never push when cooperation drops. "It is
   better to find a worse job than to make the candidate feel bad about the
   questions." This is the owner's strongest manner instruction and applies to
   every step, not only History.
2. **Required vs optional steps**, marked in the table and in
   `spec-v2-steps.json`.
3. **The step DAG** (§3.1). Each step declares inputs and outputs so the order
   can bend when a conversation wanders and the system still knows what is
   owed. The sufficiency levels become milestones derived from it.
4. **Currency is not the euro.** A profile field, set from residence at Intake,
   overridable. Every salary-equivalent figure is in the candidate's currency.
5. **Conversation language** is whatever the candidate uses; evidence keeps
   their words verbatim.
6. **Intake never produces a document.** A candidate with no CV gets the same
   structured store a parsed CV would produce. Document generation happens only
   at step 11, per ad.
7. **Traits, rewritten** (§2.2) so it is comprehensible: what a trait is, why no
   ad evidences one, and why the two-episode floor triggers a natural follow-up
   rather than a refusal to score. The stereotype judgement is internal and
   never voiced.
8. **Deletion** (§4.1) — of a whole profile, not only retraction of one fact.
9. **`last_activity` at every step boundary**, stated as a rule each step spec
   repeats.
10. **`text_sha256` normalisation** (§7.4), and an honest statement of what a
    hash cannot catch — cross-posted near-duplicates remain T13's problem.
11. **Employer replies are history** (§7.2).
12. **Outward action** (§6.2) — if an application is ever sent, a confirmation
    gate showing everything that would go. Preparing text to paste, files to
    attach, or an unsent email draft is the default.
13. **Widening the candidate's options** — unexpected job families from what was
    learned, over-confidence challenged, under-confidence encouraged, and what
    to study named. Realistic but positive.
14. **Sourcing breadth** — specialised engines, cross-border work, digital
    nomad arrangements, and how payment is arranged (taxes, legality).
15. **Understanding fallback** — what happens when an ad yields few dimensions;
    outside information about the employer, marked as not from the ad.

## The gate

Unchanged from S1: `process_spec_complete == 1`. The six required items must
still each be present and answered; the step count in `spec-v2-steps.json` must
still match the list, and S2 still divides by it. If the step count changes,
this task changes it in one place.

## Do not

Specify individual steps. Protocol detail — question wording, follow-up rules,
the manner of each step — is S2's, and the notes carry a great deal of it. This
task records the process-level rules those step specs must obey, and no more.

## Tests

The existing S1 suite, which this task extends rather than replaces —
`tests/test_process_spec.py`, run by the gate block above:

`test_the_required_steps_are_the_five_the_owner_settled` — the settled set, so a
silent change to who may be declined breaks a test rather than a candidate's
session; `test_a_process_with_no_required_step_does_not_pass` — a process every
part of which may be declined cannot put an offer in front of anyone;
`test_process_spec_answers_every_required_item` and
`test_the_settled_step_count_is_machine_readable`, unchanged, which is the point:
a revision that quietly dropped a required item or moved the count S2 divides by
would fail them.

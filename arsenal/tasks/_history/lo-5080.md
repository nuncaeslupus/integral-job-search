## What this is

The part that separates this from a form: **if something could have changed,
ask.** Three trigger kinds, evaluated at session start after identification.

| trigger | detected from | reopens |
|---------|---------------|---------|
| elapsed time | `last_activity`, and `occurred_at` on the most recent role evidence | History, Constraints, Traits |
| life event | the candidate says it — lost or left a job, moved, had a child, finished a course, changed field | the step §3.5 names for that event |
| gap | a trait below sufficiency, a constraint still `unknown`, a pending step | the step that owns the gap |

`occurred_at` is separate from `recorded_at` in the evidence row precisely so
"two years have passed since that job ended" is computable.

## The rule that matters

**A trigger produces an offer, never an action.** The tool says what it
noticed and what it would like to ask; the candidate may decline, and the
decline is recorded so it is not raised again next week.

**And it must never become an audit.** "It has been eight months, let's update
your file" is a form. "You said you left in March — how did that end up?" is a
conversation, and it is the one that gets an answer. The three named events are
examples, not the set: the general mechanism is to name the outputs the news
invalidates and reopen the steps that read them.

## What this is

Two jobs, both blocked on T23's `side` field and T25's broadened corpus:

1. **Widen the matched dimensions** so they reach families the v0 model never
   saw. Measured by `ontology_hit_rate` over the broadened corpus — the metric
   the spec already designates as the staleness signal.
2. **Add candidate-trait dimensions**: creativity, ambition, learning
   orientation (do they study their field outside work), spare-time engagement.
   These have `side: candidate_trait`, carry no cues, and are elicited only —
   the interview (T27) is their sole source.

## Do not

Write cues from imagination. Every cue needs a match in the broadened corpus,
and `test_every_dimension_has_a_cue_that_fires_on_a_real_ad` already enforces it.
If a family's vocabulary is not in the corpus, the corpus is short, not the model.

Also do not let the trait dimensions inherit the v0 gold-provenance problem
(D-2, `lo-77a6`): traits have no ad text to mine, so their evidence is episodes
from the story bank, never cue hits.

## Scope change — dimensions grow from use, 2026-08-19

The owner's decision that retired the labelling campaign (`lo-d2b2`) also says
where new dimensions come from:

> *"If, when working on a test session I can see some other dimensions to be
> added, we'll use that time to do that."*

So the widening in job (1) is no longer a batch exercise done against a corpus
sweep. A dimension is coined when a live session turns one up — the owner reads
an advert, finds something the model has no name for, and says so. That is the
same channel S11 (`lo-5530`, test mode) exists to carry, and
`talking_clients` is the worked example: coined mid-labelling, then finished
into a full dimension file with rungs, tells, three-language labels and gold.

What does **not** change: every cue still needs a match in real corpus text
(`test_every_dimension_has_a_cue_that_fires_on_a_real_ad`), and a coined
dimension is not done until it carries declared `levels`. A dimension named in
passing and left without rungs has no class set for anything to score.

`ontology_hit_rate >= 0.85` is unaffected — it is measured over ad text, not
over labels, so it keeps its denominator.

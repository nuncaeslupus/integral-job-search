Filed from a candidate session (test-mode 658fcce2). The owner's instruction for this round: the
project does a hard job to be able to sort offers, and anything that keeps it from sorting
correctly at this point is a design flaw.

## What happened

The candidate's weights price two of 41 dimensions (`mission_alignment`, `stack_modernity`). Their
traits carry evidence on thirty, including the ones they named as deciding this question: being
comfortable in the job, being able to do it without trouble, spoken English being harder for them
than written, the sectors they dislike. None of those can move an offer's position, because the
ranker only adds priced dimensions and the weights step never priced them.

## What is missing

- Every trait dimension with evidence either reaches the weights or is reported as unpriced at
  ranking time, by name — today it is silently dropped.
- A route from a stated trait ("spoken English costs me", "I dislike e-commerce") to a part-worth,
  even a coarse one, without a fresh round of choice questions for each dimension.
- A gate: `trait_dimensions_with_evidence_never_priced_and_never_reported == 0`.

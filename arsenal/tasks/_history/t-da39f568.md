Imported from issue #561

Reported by a candidate, unprompted, on 2026-09-23:

> *"veo que en algunos casos entran ofertas de shopping, de anuncios, de banca, etc. que creo que tenía descartadas por temática"*

He is right, and this is the **third** time the same candidate has raised it — the plan row for T90 already quotes his earlier diagnosis verbatim: *"are you adding filters with everything I'm telling you?"*

## Measured

`integral.sourcing_exclusions` exists, is fully tested, and T90 is ticked ☑ in `status/plan.md`. It is imported by **nothing**:

```
$ grep -rn "sourcing_exclusions" src tools | grep -v "^src/integral/sourcing_exclusions.py"
(no output)
```

Its only importer is `tests/test_sourcing_exclusions.py`. `src/integral/sourcing.py` contains the word "exclusion" twice, both in comments about unrelated logic. So the module is **dead on the serving path**: nothing constructs an `Exclusion`, nothing calls `matches()`, and no query or filter is shaped by anything the candidate ruled out.

What the candidate actually has on record, and what reached his list anyway:

| evidence | he said | what arrived |
|---|---|---|
| ev-000009 | defensa, apuestas, tabacos, bancos | banking adverts |
| ev-000026 | *"no me gusta todo lo que sea comprar y vender (e-commerce, etc.)"* | shopping adverts |
| ev-000030 | *"ya tuve bastante de fintechs"* | fintech adverts |
| ev-000031 | *"no me interesa la ciberseguridad"* | a security-engineer advert |

Applying `sourcing_exclusions.matches()` by hand with his own words to the 35 live offers of the last round knocks out **13–15 of the 35**.

## Why T90 is ticked and the defect is live

T90's gate is `restated_exclusions_resurfaced == 0`, and it passes — measured over `Candidate` rows the test constructs. The module is correct. **Nothing connects it to a search**, and the gate cannot see that, because its population is the test's fixtures rather than a real sourcing round. A module that is right and unreachable is the "being right is not being pinned" family, one step further along: here even the fixture is right.

## Two distinct halves

1. **Nothing turns evidence into `Exclusion` rows.** `Exclusion(about="<facet>:<value>", stated_at_cycle, words)` has no producer. The statements above are free text in `evidence.jsonl` and are never read as constraints.
2. **Nothing calls the module on the serving path**, so even a hand-written `Exclusion` would change no result.

Related to #552 (nothing filters a sourced offer for relevance) and distinct from it: #552 is about an advert that matches no search term, this is about an advert the candidate explicitly ruled out. An advert can pass #552's floor and still be one he said no to.

## Acceptance gate

```
stated_exclusions_not_applied_to_a_live_round == 0
```

Measured over a **real** sourcing round's offers against the candidate's own recorded statements — not over constructed `Candidate` rows, which is the denominator that let T90 pass over dead code. Pin the other direction too: an offer the candidate never excluded must still arrive, or the remedy is a quieter version of #552's over-rejection.

Note the ceiling any implementation will hit, measured on the same 35 offers: word matching gives false positives on metaphor (one advert's *"first line of defense"* reads as `defensa`) and misses consultancies whose adverts never use the word — Inetum, Grupo NS, Talent Match, AgileEngine, Napptilus, INNOCV all describe client work without naming it.

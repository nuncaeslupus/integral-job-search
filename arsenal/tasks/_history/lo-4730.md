## What this is

`profiles/<handle>/session/state.json` — current step, `position` within it,
`last_activity`, pending steps, open questions, sufficiency — plus the logic
that decides where a returning candidate lands.

Resumption order, highest first: an explicit request; a conversational cue; a
recorded position; a freshness trigger; the next step in sequence. Two rules
constrain all five — the tool **says which step it is resuming and why**, and it
never resumes into a step silently.

## The rule that matters

**State is written when something new is known, not at the end.** A session
that dies mid-interview must not lose its place; `position` is written
continuously during a step, not only at its boundary. A memory written only at
the end is not one.

`last_activity` is rewritten at every step boundary — that line belongs to every
step specification and this task is what makes it true rather than documented.

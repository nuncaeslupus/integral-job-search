## What happened

The candidate asked whether a board's emailed recommendations made sense. Reading them in his own browser found adverts and search terms the connectors had missed: a board's recommender is a second search engine run for this person. Nothing in the thirteen steps reads those emails.

## What is missing

- A section of step 7 that reads job-alert emails through the candidate's own browser session and treats each recommendation as a pointer to verify at source, never as an offer.
- Permission asked every time, naming the mailbox on screen: the browser may be signed in to another candidate's mailbox or another provider.
- The alert's own redirect links land on third-party walls; the advert is resolved at the employer's page.

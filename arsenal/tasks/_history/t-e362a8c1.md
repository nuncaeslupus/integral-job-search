Filed from a candidate session (test-mode 658fcce2). The owner's instruction for this round: the
project does a hard job to be able to sort offers, and anything that keeps it from sorting
correctly at this point is a design flaw.

## What happened

For a candidate whose recent work is building agents, harnesses and prompt tooling, `stack_fit`
reported "not on your CV: AI agents, LLM" against every AI role. The work is in their evidence log
and story bank, and the generated CVs cite it, but `cv/master.json` never received it, and
`candidate_stack` reads the master CV (plus statements) only. The fit reading is therefore wrong in
the direction that costs most: it tells the candidate they lack the very thing they do.

## What is missing

- `stack_fit` draws on every store entry a generated CV may cite, not only the master CV.
- Or the master CV is updated when the conversation adds work to the record, with the candidate's
  approval.
- A gate: a term cited by any approved generated CV for this candidate is never reported as
  `missing` by `stack_fit`.

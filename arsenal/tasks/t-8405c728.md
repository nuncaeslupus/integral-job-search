Imported from issue #683

Follow-ups from the second read of #682 (T246), comment 5970540945. Both are outside T246's stated scope.

- **One-ended pay bands are read as exact points.** "up to USD 80,000/yr", "from USD 80,000" and "$80,000" all become `PayBand(6000, 6000)`. This fails open: a ceiling is ranked as if it were a guaranteed figure.
- **`salary_recovery` currency reading:**
  - A bare `$` is read as USD, so a Canadian or Australian advert is converted at the USD rate.
  - `"CAD 80,000"` is read as no pay, so the offer is ranked as unpaid.
  - The fix should come from a closed rule, such as the ISO 4217 codes plus the advert's locale, not a hand-written list.

## The divergence

`integral.robots` and `integral.second_reader` both implement RFC 9309 §2.2.2,
and they answer differently when a rule's literal run sits behind a `*` that may
itself span the path/query delimiter. §2.2.2 orders percent-encoding "prior to
comparison"; RFC 3986 §3.3 makes `/` a segment separator inside a path and §3.4
makes it ordinary data inside a query. So a run like `/x` in `Allow: /*/x` has
**two** canonical spellings and the pattern carries no `?` to say which it means.

* **(P)** — `second_reader`'s reading. The rule is canonicalised once, as
  written; it is the **target** that is offered in extra spellings, and only to
  `Disallow` rules, on the ground that an ambiguity must not be resolved into a
  permission. Specificity therefore has one well-defined pattern length.
* **(c)** — `integral.robots`' reading since T151. A region-ambiguous run is
  emitted in **both** canonical spellings, for allows and disallows alike, and
  precedence is scored on whichever one matched. So the same rule weighs 6
  octets against a query and 4 against a path.

§2.2.2's own example table returns **Undefined** for a contest a wildcard rule
takes part in, so its text does not choose. It is entirely un-silent that a
crawler must: *"The most specific match found MUST be used."* A repository whose
two matchers choose differently has no single most-specific-match rule.

## What is already measured, and what is not

The gap is **pinned, not settled**, and this task is the settlement.

* `second_reader.REPO_MATCHER_REGRESSION_DISAGREEMENTS` names the seven
  implementer-derived cases where `integral.robots` departs from that table —
  six fail-open, one fail-closed — and
  `repo_matcher_verdicts_against_the_implementer_table` counts them in
  `status/evidence/T120.json`. Two of the seven
  (`a_wildcard_allow_does_not_outrank_a_matching_disallow`,
  `a_wildcard_allow_reaching_an_encoded_query_does_not_rescue_a_refusal`) are
  this divergence, and were opened by T151's own diff.
* `wildcard_region_spelling_readings_diverge` is the archetype, committed as a
  `LOW`-confidence row that states both readings by name and takes the
  fail-closed one, exactly as cases 24 and 25 do for (P)/(M).
* The three remaining fail-opens
  (`rules_under_an_empty_user_agent_line_are_not_dropped`,
  `a_byte_order_mark_does_not_disable_the_file`,
  `a_fragment_marker_is_data_not_a_delimiter`) are **not** this divergence —
  they are parser defects in `integral.robots` that the widened measurement
  surfaced. They belong to this task only in that nothing was measuring them
  either; split them out if that reads better once the reading is settled.

**Scale, measured rather than asserted — and measured three times, by three
generators that do not share a population.**

| generator | triples | disagree | skew toward `robots.py` |

| the independent second reader on #422 | 1,836 | **150 (8.2%)** | 7:1 |

The rate depends on how wildcard-heavy the generator is and the two figures
bracket it rather than contradicting each other; what neither is, is small, and
what neither was, is visible from any committed metric before this pin. **A
third figure — 91 of 4,830 — was reported by an earlier attempt at this filing
whose generator was lost when the session was killed. It is deliberately not
recorded here: an unreproducible number in a task file is the kind of evidence
this repository keeps legislating against.** The settlement must produce its own
denominator anyway, per point 4 below.

## What the task must produce

1. **Pick one reading, in writing**, with the derivation from §2.2.2, RFC 3986
   §3.3/§3.4 and §2.2.3 — not from what either module currently does. Deciding
   correctness by running the code is the circularity the second-reader rule
   exists to break.
2. **Record the losing branch's risk beside the choice.** (P) refuses more than
   the RFC requires when a site writes an `Allow` behind a `*`; (c) permits more
   when it writes one, and makes a rule's weight against a fixed competitor move
   with the request. Both costs are real and the choice is which to pay.
3. **Make both modules take it**, or state explicitly why one deliberately does
   not and gate that statement.
4. **A measurement, not a claim.** `integral.matcher_readings` runs both matchers
   over a generated population of contested triples and reports
   `matcher_reading_divergences_unrecorded` — divergences that are neither
   eliminated nor named in a committed contested row. The triples compared are
   the denominator, asserted as a floor in the `naming.MINIMUM_SCANNED` style
   (T100) so a shrunken generator cannot score a clean zero, and
   `gate_status: unmeasured` when the generator yields nothing.

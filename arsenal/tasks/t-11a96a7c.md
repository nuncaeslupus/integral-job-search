Filed from #598's second readers (round 1 "observation, not blocking"; round 2 F2). T218 checks only `STATEMENT_STEPS = ("identify", "constraints")` (`src/integral/sourcing_exclusions.py`). But T203's own complaint was about topics the candidate ruled out *in words, wherever said*. A refusal written to `profile/evidence.jsonl` under any other step is never listed by `unrecorded_statements`, never reaches step 7's `exclusion_backfill_warning`, and so never becomes an `Exclusion`. That fails open: the candidate keeps seeing the topic.

Inputs the second readers measured as unlisted on T218's head (all fixtures):

| step | row text | required |
|---|---|---|
| `feedback` (step 10) | "no me enseñéis más bancos" | listed |
| `reactions` (step 5) | "nada de apuestas" | listed |
| `preferences` (step 6) | "no quiero consultoras" | listed |
| `sourcing` (step 7) | a topic ruled out mid-search that step 7's SKILL.md says to `record` first, and that was not recorded | listed |

## What to decide and build

- **Which steps, and which rows in them.** A `reaction` row about one advert ("this one is boring") is not a topic exclusion. A row of the same kind that rules out a sector is one. Derive which kinds and steps state a topic from `status/spec-v2-steps.md` and each step's SKILL.md, not from the cue regex. Keep the fail-open weighting: an over-read costs one line of review, a missed row costs a topic the candidate keeps seeing.
- **Do not widen the cue to compensate.** T218's rule is that every `constraint` row counts and the cue gates only the other kinds. Extending that to more steps means a cue-gated population much larger than before. Measure how many rows of the new steps a fixture profile lists that state no topic, and say whether the loop can still reach exit 0.
- **Step 10's privacy rule still holds.** A rejection reason given in feedback is never quoted to an employer. Recording it as an `Exclusion` keeps it in the candidate's own `search/exclusions.json`, which is not an application document. Confirm nothing in step 11 reads that file.

Builds on T221 (`t-bfc977c6`), which changes the same predicate and closing rule. Do this after it.

## The defect

T159's rule is that a floor either sits where the first deletion breaches it, or
**states the margin it keeps and why**. The second half is checked by matching
keywords in the comment above the declaration. So the check is satisfiable by
writing a sentence.

Measured on #436 by the second reader, five rounds in:

> **73 of 73 floors are settable to `1` with the gate exiting 0**, if the same
> commit replaces the comment with *"The margin here is deliberate."*

**And that is the realistic mutation.** T159's own metric changes a floor's value
while holding its comment fixed — nobody lowers a floor that way. Every number
that task reported (33 of 67, then 30 of 76, then 26 of 73) is an artefact of
mutating the value alone; under the mutation a real regression would make, the
figure was 73 of 73 in every round.

`_claimed_margin_size` also implements "states the margin" while dropping the
spec's **"and why"**.

## Why this one comes first

Every other number in the floor census is conditional on it. A floor reported as
compliant because its comment says so is not evidence of anything, so no count of
compliant floors means anything until a margin is asserted by something a commit
cannot satisfy in prose.

## What the metric counts

`floors_cleared_by_prose_alone` is the number of committed floors that the sweep
reports compliant, and that remain reported compliant when the floor's value is
lowered **and** its comment is rewritten to any text asserting deliberateness.

That is the mutation to implement: **value and prose together**, because that is
what a careless commit actually does.

The floors swept are the denominator, committed as a **literal** floor per T122.

**Verify the metric is non-zero against today's tree before writing the fix** —
against #436's head it should read 73.

## The direction, from the reader that measured it

A margin must be **a marker, not a keyword** — emitted rather than typed, in the
idiom `review_reader` already uses for a second-reader report, so that a commit
cannot mint one by writing a sentence. What the marker attests, and whether it
carries the population it was computed against, is the design question this task
settles.

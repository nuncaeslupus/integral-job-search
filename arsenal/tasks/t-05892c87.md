Captured during a test session at step `constraints` (ledger `test-2026-08-20-a`).

> About "falso autónomo", this is a bad thing, not something someone wants to work of. Maybe you
> should have asked: "Are you autónomo?", or rather, given he is looking for a job, "Would you
> consider to become autónomo or you prefer payroll?"

The session asked whether the candidate would take work "as autónomo/falso autónomo". Falso autónomo
is an illegal arrangement, not an employment mode on a menu. The `employment_mode` question must be
phrased as a status check or a preference, never as an option being offered.

Addressed in: `step-02-constraints`.

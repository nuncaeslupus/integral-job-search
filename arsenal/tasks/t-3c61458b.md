Filed from #598's second reader (round 3, F6 — non-blocking, fail-closed).

`sourcing_exclusions.unrecorded_statements` (T218) lists every `constraint`-kind row of steps `identify` / `constraints` whatever its words, and skips only step 2's own write of `candidate.CONSTRAINT_FIELD_NAMES`. A constraint row tagged with any other dimension — `dimensions=["commute"]`, "No quiero desplazarme más de 30 minutos.", the shape of `integral.feedback`'s own fixture — rules out no topic, no honest `record` can cover it, and it is listed forever. So `python -m integral.sourcing_exclusions unrecorded` never exits 0 on such a profile, and step 7's "Backfill" bullet says both "re-run until it exits 0" and "leave such a row as it is".

## Rule

A per-row **acknowledgement** in `search/exclusion_acknowledgements.json` — "this row was read with the candidate and rules out no kind of work" — that `unrecorded_statements` honours. It must never be able to silence a topic row, so it is honoured only when **all** hold, re-checked on every read (not only at write):

1. **Per row, never per dimension.** It names one `evidence_id`; a later row with the same dimension is still listed.
2. **Only the candidate's words.** Its `words` equal the live row's `text` byte for byte; it has no reason or note field, so the session has nowhere to put a judgement of its own. A row restated, corrected or retracted no longer matches and is listed again.
3. **Only a row that says what it is about.** The row carries at least one dimension, and the acknowledgement names one of them. An **untagged** constraint row is never acknowledgeable: that is the bare topic list ("defensa, apuestas, tabacos, bancos") this check exists for, and nothing tells it from a pay floor — fail-closed by choice.
4. **Never a topic facet.** No dimension of the row is the facet of a recorded exclusion (`Exclusion.facet`) — the profile's own record of which facets are topics.

Ceiling, stated as T218 states its own: a row tagged only with a non-topic dimension that also names a topic in passing ("no más de 30 minutos, y nada de bancos") can be acknowledged. The skill asks for such a topic as its own `record` first, the same answer T218 gives a topic said inside a pinned field's quote.

`unrecorded` exit 0 then means: every listed row was recorded or acknowledged. Step 7's SKILL.md "Backfill" bullet is rewritten so its loop is the one the CLI closes, and names the untagged-row ceiling.
